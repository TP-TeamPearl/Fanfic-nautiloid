## tadpole Vs. Serum
Despite the installed systems, [[CH_00]]  could not properly synchronize with the vessel’s psionic controls. The failure was attributed primarily to the unstable bridging technology developed by the human engineering team rather than to the captain himself. 
Gortash authorized a new phase of testing centered on [[CH_00]] personally.

The captain was given a serum described as an experimental catalyst designed to unlock latent cognitive potential and accelerate compatibility with psionic systems. The treatment was presented as a privilege—an opportunity for him to become the first human capable of commanding illithid technology directly. 

**The serum itself was a psionic nullifier.**

While [[CH_00]] continued the regimen under medical supervision, he was summoned repeatedly to the laboratory for observation. During one of these visits he was secretly implanted with an illithid tadpole, and the memory of the procedure was removed. The treatment continued afterward, though the dosage was gradually reduced and replaced in part with a harmless placebo.

As the tadpole began establishing its influence, [[CH_00]] slowly developed the psionic sensitivity needed to perceive the vessel’s systems and the minds of the illithids aboard. To him, the change confirmed the success of the serum. By the time the “treatment” was declared complete, [[CH_00]] believed he had become the first successful human interface with illithid technology.

The serum served as a psionic nullifier. Prior to infection it had no measurable impact, but once the tadpole was implanted it suppressed the abrupt psionic awakening normally induced by ceremorphosis. The result was a controlled and incremental emergence of abilities, allowing the illusion that Havrok’s progress stemmed from the treatment rather than from the parasite itself.
