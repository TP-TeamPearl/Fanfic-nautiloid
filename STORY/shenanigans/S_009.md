## S_09A Crate  
(bound to [[S_008]])

### Phase_0
[[CH_08]] has established enough contact with [[CI_00]] to reopen their old working arrangement. The Emperor asks for something specific: a crate of XXX (surface materials, tools, or components unavailable or inconvenient to obtain from the deeper colony. It gives little explanation.
CHECK! The content will be revealed later so it should be something that shows the Emperor resourcefulness and ability to think ahead)

[[CH_08]] secures the requested crate and learns through the Banite workers that [[CI_00]] has been assigned to a departing nautiloid with human operators.

When they next meet, [[CH_08]] may ask:
> - So you're leaving the colony? You're going to build something afar?
> - I am intended to return here once the mission succeeds.

 [[CH_08]] had expected to find a way to extract it from the colony, but the departure creates a simpler possibility: once outside Moonrise, getting away from the Absolute's range may be considerably easier than getting it out by force. They therefore should press for clarification, like:
> - Are you really coming back here? You're not going to expend the Absolute's cult elsewhere? 
[[CI_00]] confirms that the mission is temporary and that it is intended to return once it has succeeded.
> - You will have access to very valuable prototype machinery. I know your nautiloid carries a human scientific crew. Suppose I wanted to come aboard?

Their old efficiency does not guarantee compatibility aboard the vessel. It can recognize the opportunity while simultaneously seeing an unknown variable. 
*More importantly, CI_00 has already secured a prestigious position as  [[CI_00## Retcon:]] and gained direct access to the command structure. It has little need for a human subordinate and no reason to complicate its position by claiming responsibility for another crew member.*

> - You would not be under my authority.
> - Is that regret, or rejection? I am glad to have found you here, and I would be glad to pursue what we had. But I will not keep fuelling it alone.
> - If you board, I will not object. I may even support your application, should you be judged worthy enough to become part of the crew.

"support" or something ambiguous, it should mean anything from mentioning CH_08 to the relevant Banites, endorsing their work, vouching for their usefulness, or simply ensuring the application reaches the right people. [[CH_08]] may reasonably interpret it as a meaningful intervention even if [[CI_00]] actually has little control over the decision.

* [[CI_00]] cannot directly appoint [[CH_08]], but it can lend its recommendation to the application. Their prior work will still be reviewed by the Banites, likely with an initial assessment by Ketheric's cultists. The Emperor's support therefore increases the likelihood of acceptance without guaranteeing it.*

The Emperor should **present a limited influence as a favour with greater weight than it objectively possesses**.
- Did [[CI_00]] arrange this because it wanted CH_08 aboard, or because they happened to be the most efficient available solution? 
  *To be Emperor-like, it should not produce a clean answer.*

After that, they do not need to suddenly become close again aboard the ship. The continuity is already demonstrated by this event.
