# General rules:

- The project takes place before the events of the canonical Baldur's Gate 3 campaign. Where the project invents events that are not represented in the game, those inventions should avoid creating contradictions, unexplained knowledge, impossible chronology, or memories that conflict with later canonical dialogue and character experiences.
	Additionally, the core team would like to avoid interfering with in-game interpretations by filling in gaps the game deliberately leaves open (tadpoler's identity, Stelmane …) or by altering the meaning of canon scenes through our shenanigans.
