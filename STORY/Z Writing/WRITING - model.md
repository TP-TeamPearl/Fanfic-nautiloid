# Header

- Ambiguity belongs to the presentation, not to the writer's understanding.

Headers are always factual, they encompass the characters'secrets and the way they understand the situation, their intentions and their current status (mind and health). these elements are the fixed base to replay a scene from different angles.

## Use this as a helper.
