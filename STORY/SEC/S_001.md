# S_001 Infrastructure 
Human crew [[L_54 Bio-technical Workshop# FH_03 Water Filtration Assembly|Water]]
Before departure, several crew members reportedly objected to drinking directly from the vessel's water systems, concerned that water passing through the nautiloid's living tissues or unknown materials might contain particles or substances harmful to human metabolism. Pressure from the crew eventually resulted in the installation of an independent filtration system.

The filtration assembly is therefore partly a practical safeguard and partly a matter of trust. There is no established evidence that the illithid process is harmful to humans, but the crew has preferred a system whose operation and maintenance they can understand themselves.
