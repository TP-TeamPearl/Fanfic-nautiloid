### Procedure
[[CI_05]] performs the modification as a continuous precision procedure, closer to an industrial assembly arm than a conventional surgeon. Its movements are chained and uninterrupted: position, open, insert, align, secure, move to the next task… It does not pause to assess the subject's distress and expects CI_04 to handle it. 

[[CI_04]] keeps the subject conscious and compliant through low-cost psionic regulation rather than heavy spellwork. It does not simply speak reassuringly into the subject's mind, but overlays reality with sensations associated with safety and affection: warmth, a familiar touch, the feeling of a mother's kiss, fragments of forgotten comfort. These memories are not an illusion replacing the laboratory; they coexist with it, the subject is fully aware. CI_04 uses whatever emotional associations best stabilize the subject, exploiting them without hesitation so the procedure can continue.

**Ending Procedure**
Once the physical work is complete, [[CI_05]] disengages (letting [[CH_06]] clean the tools), but [[CI_04]] does not immediately withdraw. It keeps the subject calm for a little longer, allowing the sensations of warmth, familiarity and protection to persist while the body settles. The reassurance is no longer needed for the procedure itself; CI_04 is reinforcing the association it has just created, making its presence something the subject can begin to rely upon.

Then it retreats from the subject's mind without warning. The sensations disappear at once, leaving the subject with the laboratory, its altered body, and the realization that the comfort was never theirs to keep. CI_04 leaves without explanation.
