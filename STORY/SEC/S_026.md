# S_026A
[[CI_04]] is somehow coerced to board with captives not properly enthralled.

## Context
Human intervention at Moonrise has consequences beyond the direct extraction of resources. Gortash's increasingly aggressive launch schedule, driven by the search for the Astral Prism, compresses the colony's procedures and forces illithid operations to conform to human priorities.
This creates cumulative degradation rather than isolated mistakes. The consequences become particularly dangerous during the final preparations for departure. 

[[CI_04]] is therefore coerced into boarding with captives who are not properly enthralled. Not an error in illithid expertise, but a consequence of a process that has been progressively degraded and then forced past its normal safety margin. The captives remain under partial control, but their conditioning is incomplete, creating a latent instability that may not become apparent until after departure.
