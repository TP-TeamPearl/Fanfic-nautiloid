[[CH_02]] takes [[CH_00]] on a tour of the ship to show him the systems and machines.

# S_084A
During the initial inspection, CH_00 raises concerns about the ship's dependence on illithid personnel for operating its security and emergency systems. Several controls require psionic interaction or illithid authorization, leaving the human crew unable to respond independently when the responsible illithid is absent or incapacitated.

# S_084B
He insists that critical emergency procedures must remain accessible to qualified human personnel, particularly during situations where immediate intervention cannot wait for an illithid operator.

The request encounters some resistance, as the nautiloid was not designed to accommodate a human crew. Nevertheless, a limited adaptation is approved for the fire-suppression system.

---

*Both the Security Office (L_41) and the Thrall Overseer's Office (L_18A) can trigger the fire-suppression system, and both locations are equally inconvenient to access.*

The necessary modifications require [[CH_04]] (or [[CI_02]] but it is not cooperative) to establish connections between the consoles, install additional cabling and repeatedly test the new interface…

→ CI_02, keen to avoid having engineers and their equipment cluttering the Security Office, makes sure that the modifications and subsequent testing are directed towards the secondary console in CI_04's office instead. 

CI_04 consequently inherits the entire operation. Accustomed to silent thralls, it now has to accommodate CH_04 (and potentially CH_07) who has opinions, questions, technical requirements and perhaps even little inclination to follow its preferred working arrangements.

→ Given the office's elevated position, CI_04 may even have to levitate the engineers and their equipment up to it. It must then tolerate their repeated interventions, requests for access, cable adjustments and increasingly tedious functionality tests until the installation is considered stable.

# S_084C
- System is now functional.

Following CH_00's request, the secondary fire-control console in CI_04's office receives a limited adaptation allowing designated human personnel to activate its emergency functions without psionic authentication.
