# Introduction - CH_02

- First reaction should be an inquiry, not an accusation

CH_00 discovers the animal, follows it, finds CH_02 (uncertain if he's a crew member), and asks about CH_02's identity then introduce himself. That would give a professional and courteous entry
before raising the issue(s). He can ask about CH_02's assignment, perhaps show some interest in his work, and then return to the PET: (whose animal it is and) why it is aboard. CH_02 would explain that it is his companion and working partner. The captain then would ask an obvious question as: "Why wasn't I informed that an animal was being brought aboard?"
→ Gortash certainly already allowed CH_02 to board with the PET if CI_02 didn't care.
## What would it show about the captain
DISCUSS!
Someone who values order, routine and clear chains of responsibility. He could be impatient, especially in such a case, when he discovers that decisions have been made around him. He may occasionally use courtesy to conceal irritation, and he is conscious of how he appears before subordinates. But maybe not the kind looking for opportunities to punish people.

He needs the human crew to accept his authority voluntarily, or at least to consider it worth cooperating with. He should be conscious that he cannot afford to turn every disagreement into a confrontation. He may be particularly careful with CH_02 because the ranger has a useful assignment, is physically capable, and is not easily intimidated.
