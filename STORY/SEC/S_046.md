The Jammers are activated, placing the nautiloid in a very different operational state. The ship's systems are running at minimal output, while the illithids' psionic abilities are heavily restricted.

This configuration must be accounted for throughout the sequence, until the Gith establish their "lock" on the ship. Once the ship is locked, any attempt at concealment or dissimulation becomes impossible.

Basically: all machinery operates at minimal output, and the illithids' psionics are mostly disabled.
