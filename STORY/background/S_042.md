
# HELPER — Moonrise

- [[CH_08]] infiltrates Moonrise with one primary objective: **locate and extract [[CI_00]]**.

They already know the Emperor survived and is being held somewhere within Gortash's operation. At first, they lack the information needed to understand the scale of what they have entered.

*The upper and working areas of the towers are not completely sealed. Banite workers and non-tadpoled cultists occasionally speak openly about the captured mind flayer that they dragged there from Baldur's Gate and about the Absolute being an elder brain rather than a deity. These fragments gradually confirm that [[CI_00]] is alive, controlled, and far more deeply embedded in the operation than expected.*

### Deepening the Cover

[[CH_08]]'s magical training, particularly in wizardry and necromantic practices, allows them to gain access deeper into the towers under the appearance of a useful specialist. 

- Their cover becomes unexpectedly attractive.

They are learning things they could not have learned elsewhere and operating in an environment where their methods encounter almost no ethical resistance. They are still trying to extract CI_00, but the cover starts to feel less like a disguise and more like **the best apprenticeship they have ever been given**.

- As they learn more, extracting someone—especially an illithid—from this place becomes increasingly implausible.

Eventually, they acquire enough information/gossip from other workers to descend deeper into the colony and approach [[CI_00]] directly.

- [[CH_08]] believes their infiltration remains concealed. The elder brain already knows their intentions.

The elder brain has already manipulated Gortash through dreams into sending [[CI_00]] on the upcoming mission. A separate individual attempting to free it is therefore potentially useful rather than dangerous. [[CH_08]] is allowed to continue, they are another piece on its board.

Their plan is deliberately gradual:
 - locate [[CI_00]] > establish contact > gain access and freedom of movement > create an opportunity to bring it nearer to the surface > extract it when circumstances permit.
They assume they have enough time to build that opportunity. [[CI_00]] does not share that premise. 

[[CI_00]] knows the upcoming mission is dangerous and may very well be its last. Its immediate objective is therefore much narrower: **secure anything useful to survival aboard the nautiloid, increase its leverage over the crew, and place itself in the strongest possible position within the mission.**
