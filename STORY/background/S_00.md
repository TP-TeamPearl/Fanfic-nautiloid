# Solving a problem of scarcity

- [[CI_03]] has been cultivating exceptional prey inside the permitted boundaries of the colony's hierarchy.

*Shar's curse has deprived the colony of the occasional wizards, scholars, and other exceptional minds that once made for refined prey. CI_03 adapted by **cultivating promising minds itself**.
The disappearance of [[PHASE 0 - The Colony#^disap|Balthazar]] created an opportunity. With oversight weakened and the Bhaalists habitually blamed for disappearances, CI_03 could work more freely.*
What makes the practice particularly satisfying is that the final brain contains not only the target's original potential, but the memories of what CI_03 helped them become.

- **Find a target**  
    CI_03 identifies workers with unusual cognitive or creative potential and flatters them by recognizing abilities others overlook. *The praise is genuine, which makes it more effective.*
- **Refine the target's mind**  
    It gains their trust through instruction: psionic methods, occult techniques, and new ways of approaching their own craft. The exchange is often mutually beneficial, with CI_03 requesting minor tools, components, or assistance in return.
- **Induce an infraction**  
    CI_03 cannot directly violate the protocols protecting workers of the Absolute. Instead, it exploits their ambition or ignorance to push them toward a prohibited action, causing them to lose that protection and become [[Protocols# Workers protection|legally vulnerable]].
- **Catch the vulnerable**  
    Once stripped of protection, the target can be isolated, detained, and ultimately consumed. Their disappearance can be attributed to the Bhaalists or the general disorder of the colony.
