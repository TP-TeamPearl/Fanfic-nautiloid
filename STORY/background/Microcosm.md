# CH_06

An open amphitheater beneath a clear, sunlit sky. Magical projections of anatomical notes drift through the air and fall like autumn leaves, each page covered in diagrams, measurements, and delicate annotations.

[[CH_06]] approaches the center of the stage. On the table lies a patient, an undefined human figure, calm and motionless. Her finger lightly tends the radial artery in the wounded wrist. A soft note sounds at each pulse and each sound becomes a color. 

The scalpel in [[CH_06]]’s hand responds to the music, its metal reshaping itself with each tone. The blade softens and unfolds into a fine, needle-like instrument. She begins to knit the vessels together as a deeper state of meditation. 

Luminescent pearls of blood detach from the patient, floating and ascending in the air. Around her, the anatomical notes continue to descend. Nothing splashes. Nothing tears beyond what is strictly necessary.

As she works, the threads of blood vessels extend outward and upward, joining one another in increasingly intricate patterns. They form a luminous dome above the amphitheater: a curved anatomical lattice of fine lines and suspended points of light. The blood droplets become markers moving through the network, pulsing in sequence with the patient's heartbeat.
The dome becomes a living diagram of the body, its circulation translated into music and geometry.
