### First Meeting
[[CH_08]] eventually reaches [[CI_00]] while wearing the robe of Ketheric's workers. They acknowledge its current authority and condition without treating enthrallment as a reason to discard its judgement. They approach it as they would have approached their former associate through correspondence: with an edge of familiarity, measured deference, and the assumption that the underlying relationship still exists. Their behaviour merely communicates "This is not over".

From [[CI_00]]'s perspective, [[CH_08]] may be:
- genuinely attempting to restore their former arrangement
- using it as a useful contact to gain access and influence within the colony (exploiting it as an old professional relationship)
- acting under instructions from elsewhere (testing whether it is genuinely enthralled)

-> The relationship must survive the loss of context, that's all we need to push further.

[[CI_00]] cannot directly appoint [[CH_08]], but it can lend its recommendation to the application. Their prior work will still be reviewed by the Banites, likely with an initial assessment by Ketheric's cultists. The Emperor's support therefore increases the likelihood of acceptance without guaranteeing it.
