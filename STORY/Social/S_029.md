# Hair brushing
[[CH_08]] leaves the deposit [[L_24A The Deposit]] after she inspected the contents of several crates before returning to her cell.

As [[CH_08]] rounds the passage to the deposit [[L_24A The Deposit]], she catches sight of [[CI_04]] ([[L_22D Thrall Sleeping Platform]]). As she finishes to inspect the contents of several crates and leaves the deposit and returns to her cell, she sees several thralls waiting in a loose line nearby [[CI_04]] that is seating on a low stool.

One thrall sits on the floor directly in front of [[CI_04]], perfectly still, while it slowly brushes their hair. [[CI_04]] is simply using its free time, it is the end of the day's work for it and its thralls. The scene should feel oddly domestic.

[[CI_04]] thralls' hair are longer than that of most illithid servants since they are all shaved as a matter of routine, both for cleanliness and to reduce the risk of parasites. [[CI_04]], however, has special authorization to keep some of its thralls groomed rather than shaved (they remain properly maintained). It takes obvious pride in this distinction.
*The same exception extends to the human crew, who protested loudly enough against compulsory shaving that an accommodation was eventually granted.*

[[CH_08]] stops for a moment and quietly watches, commenting as it's finishing the last thrall.
>They're lucky someone takes care of them.

[[CI_04]] pauses just a beat, looking at her, then at her hair.
> Neglect is such an ugly habit.

[[CH_08]] 
>What about brushing mine? I'm too tired to discuss it anyway. You won't make the difference with them.

It does not answer immediately. It finishes with the last thrall in front of it. Then after they left, it performs the same mechanical gesture with its hand. CH_08 approaches silently and takes their place before it.
