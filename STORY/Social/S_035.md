# S_035B

Old cables pass through or disappear into [[L_59 Unresolved Growth]]. Nobody knows what the growth is connected to.

[[CH_04]] becomes obsessed with determining where the old cables lead. He traces them through inaccessible spaces, strips away their coverings, tests continuity, and follows junctions into increasingly degraded sections of the vessel. With every contact, he is exposed further to the vessel's eldritch emanations, and his sanity deteriorates accordingly.
He is an engineer. After the first warning signs, he keeps going.
## Crisis
For a few seconds, CH_04's perception breaks. He hears voices from places that should be nowhere near him. Distances collapse. Different layers of the ship seem to overlap. Machinery appears to connect across impossible spaces. He experiences fata morgana-like visions and sounds that cannot have a physical source.
[[CH_04]] collapses, rolls across the floor, and screams. The crew is unable to bring him back to a stable state and places him in an artificial coma.

They will have to wait for Gortash's next remote meeting before deciding what to do with him.

**This event permanently damages CH_04.
All interactive scenes involving CH_04 must predate this event.**
