## Assignment
[[CH_07]] was ordered to join the expedition as part of the scientific complement.
[[CH_06]] volunteered to accompany him despite not being required. [[CH_07]] initially refused, considering the nautiloid and its illithid crew too dangerous.
She approached the Banites herself and secured permission to join the expedition anyway.

# Phase_1
## Maintenance 1 & 2

[[CH_04]] visits [L_32A The infirmary](<L_32A The infirmary.md>) looking for [[CH_07]], but finds only [[CH_06]].

They discuss the equipment currently in use. [[CH_04]] realises that [[CH_06]] and [[CH_07]] have been operating several preserved illithid devices without fully understanding their internal functions. He cannot explain the machinery either, but he recognises the parts that still follow familiar engineering principles. Instead of continuing to service them for her, he teaches her the practical maintenance he knows: cleaning contact surfaces, checking fittings, replacing simple components, tightening and aligning mechanical parts, and following the standard startup and shutdown sequence.

The point is not to teach her what the devices *do*, but how to keep them functional without depending on him.

[[CH_06]] can now maintain the equipment herself and continue experimenting with it independently.

---

Later, [[CI_05]] enters the infirmary personally, an unusual occurrence. Several specimens have been returned contaminated by an unsuitable serum, making them unusable for the next stage of the laboratory's procedures.

There, [[CI_05]] notices that [[CH_06]] is not merely operating an illithid device: she has learned to maintain and correctly calibrate it.
*At this stage, humanoids can use some illithid equipment under restricted conditions, but maintenance remains beyond most of them.*

[[CI_05]] offers [[CH_06]] access to its laboratory during her free time, where she may work with more advanced equipment.

*The offer is entirely practical. [[CI_05]] has no particular interest in encouraging her development for its own sake; it simply prefers equipment that can be operated and maintained correctly without requiring its intervention afterward.*
