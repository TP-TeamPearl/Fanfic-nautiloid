# The hunt operates as a distributed process:
- Bound to: [[L_07 Intake Chamber]]

([[CI_05]] should have already established the required subject profile and transmitted it to [[CI_04]])

**CI_01 → identification and interpretation**
[[CI_01]] detect available targets on the radars and transmits their profile to [[CI_00]]. Potentially dangerous signals are redirected to [[CI_02]].

**CI_02 → security**
CI_02 checks the target/environment from the security perspective: hazards, hostile signals, anomalous activity, interference, etc.

**CI_00 → reachability**
[[CI_00]] evaluates whether the Nautiloid can actually reach and take the target.

**CI_04 → final subject selection**
Once a target is sufficiently validated, the information reaches [[CI_04]], which decides whether that particular individual is worth taking according to the already-established requirements.
