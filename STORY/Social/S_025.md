## "Ma "and "Pa"

CH_02 or CH_04 asks a simple question:
>What are your names?

>**Ma.**
>**Pa.**

That should initially sound absurd, almost childlike. They examine them more carefully: Human and kuo-toa traits (like Innsmouth Lovecraft).
Then the friends suspect that those aren't names in the ordinary sense. They are a designation of function: these two have likely been bred to reproduce.

Then a pair like this doesn't imply a short voyage. It implies **expected duration**
- creates paranoia

The truth, the illithids always prepare for contingency. So there are rational explanations but we shouldn't mention them.
It must feel like
>Someone prepared the ship as though the future could be much longer, larger, and more uncertain than we were told.
