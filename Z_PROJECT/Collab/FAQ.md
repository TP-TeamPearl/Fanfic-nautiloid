
**Q: Do I have to use Git pull/push?**
**A:** It would make things easier for the core team, but a lot of Markdown files and discussion contributions are still exchanged by mail.

**Q: Can I contribute in languages other than English?**
**A:** No. Use Reverso or a random AI to translate your work before sharing it.

**Q: When will the actual writing phase begin?**
**A:** Probably not before the end of 2027. The construction of events is an iterative process: previous, interconnected events often need to be adjusted in response to character actions, while many of those characters are still very much under development.

**Q: Is there a social group where I can join and discuss things directly?**
**A:** No, not at the moment. This is mainly because our previous projects involved only three members, with a handful of occasional contributors. We could set up a Discord server, but none of us currently feels particularly inclined or available to moderate one.

**Q: Is there romance?**
**A:** Romance is an important part of Baldur's Gate 3, but it is not - and will never be - a priority for this fic. You're welcome to develop it if you want, though. 
The core team is not particularly concerned with representation or inclusivity quotas, and the current cast is mainly composed of men, so you'll have to gender-swap existing characters or introduce more female ones if you don't want it to be "woke". 

**Q: Is there a TAV?**
**A:** No. We're trying to distribute the interesting ideas and interactions we'd like to explore across all our characters. For the same reason, while you're free to "self-insert", it's not recommended, as anyone can edit what you write. The closest equivalent to a "Tav" is, in some ways, the Emperor, as it will be present from the beginning and survive the entire journey.

**Q: What if I disagree with your view of the Emperor?**
**A:** Our goal is to remain faithful to its philosophy and echo its narrative construction. These are things that can be factually checked, so you're more than welcome to correct us. What we all want here is "the Emperor", not a deviant version that merely shares its name.

**Q: What if I don't care about certain characters or want to harm them?**
**A:** Aside from the captain and the duties interactions required, nothing really forces your character(s) to interact with anyone in particular. You're also free to act with hostility, but there should be consequences if they get caught.

**Q: What if I have ideas that would considerably change the layout of the nautiloid's rooms?**
**A:** Your ideas will be heard, but there's no guarantee they'll be implemented. This is especially true if you can't edit the existing 3D mockup yourself or produce comparable screenshots using another piece of software you're more comfortable with. In this case, the best approach is probably to send a mail before investing time in the work.

**Q: Do the adventure rules strictly follow 5e?**
**A:** We try to stick to the rules and lore as they stood before BG3, and then follow DnD 5e more loosely. Changes and retcons are expected, particularly regarding the Spelljammer setting (still under discussion). The core team also considers that part of the essence of DnD is finding creative ways to bend the rules and exploit the intended or unintended uses of existing abilities to solve problems.

**Q: Can my character die?**
**A:** At the beginning of the canon adventure, only the Larian cast is still alive: the Emperor, the dying illithid (whom we call CI_04), and the heroes. Any deviation from this starting point will necessarily result in a fork.

**Q: Are there any subjects or themes that are off-limits?**
**A:** The game is rated 18+, so theoretically, no. What matters is staying within the "spirit" of the game and DnD, while respecting the rules of the platforms on which our work is published or shared.
*For example, we don't use the story to spread political propaganda or to shape the consequences of characters' actions around the delivery of a moral lesson.*

**Q: I'm interested, but there are things I'm concerned about...**
**A:** Send us a mail. Your concerns and suggestions will be heard.

**Q: Who makes the decisions about the project's overall direction?**
**A:** St. If you want to get deeply involved in every phase, he's the person you should get in touch with.
