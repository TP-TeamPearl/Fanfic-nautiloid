# L_23A Illithid Kitchen
Cognitive Processing Chamber: The chamber is not continuously staffed. A designated thrall is assigned to the room but is present only when processing is required. Subjects deemed no longer useful for labor are transferred here. 
Exceptional minds are extracted intact and preserved in sealed cerebrospinal containment jars. Residual subjects, whose cognitive structures no longer meet minimum thresholds, are rendered into nutrient mass.
The room remains freely accessible, although a curtain across the entrance prevents the human crew from having a direct view of the gore inside.
