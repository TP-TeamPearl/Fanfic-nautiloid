# L_26A Engines Room (Lower)
This lower part of the Engine Room [[L_26D Engines Room|L_26D]] is considerably longer than the machinery space above it. A large water container occupies part of the chamber and surrounds the descending engine assemblies. Mechanical boards and service panels line the walls. 
The room contains a furnace. Its presence is therefore considered unrelated to the current function of the room.
A psionic door permits the access but is placed high under the ceiling.

- Source: Waterdeep: Dungeon of the Mad Mage
