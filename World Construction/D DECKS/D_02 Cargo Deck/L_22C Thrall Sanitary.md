# L_22C Thrall Sanitary
A communal washing and sanitary area serving the cargo-deck thralls.
Open washing basins line one wall, while simple floor-level latrines are arranged without partitions or privacy. The layout permits several occupants to use the facilities simultaneously, prioritising efficiency over personal dignity. 
## Inventory:
- Stone wash basins
- Floor-level latrines
- Water channels
- Drainage grates
