# L_22A Thrall Common Room
The only entrance to this interconnected accommodation block opens into this room. Rather than enclosed by conventional walls, the compartment is bounded by a thick organic barrier that resembles a solid fence, allowing the surrounding area to remain visible while preventing unrestricted movement. The ceiling is unusually low, forming the floor of the sleeping platform above [[L_22D Thrall Sleeping Platform|L_22D]], which is reached by an external ladder.
Long stone tables occupy the centre of the room, accompanied by crude shelving and wooden crates used as stools. The arrangement resembles a communal refectory, providing a place where cargo-deck thralls may gather during designated rest periods. 
Unlike prisoners or newly captured labourers, the resident thralls require neither cells nor continuous confinement, their obedience rendering such measures unnecessary.
