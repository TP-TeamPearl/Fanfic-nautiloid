# L_22B Former Dormitory
Originally intended as an extension of the thralls' sleeping quarters, this room was briefly repurposed by members of the human crew during the vessel's preparation. More comfortable tables and chairs were installed in the mistaken belief that it was intended as their mess hall.
Once the true nature of the workers became apparent, the human crew abandoned the room and established improved facilities in [[L_05 Kitchen (crew)|L_05]].
