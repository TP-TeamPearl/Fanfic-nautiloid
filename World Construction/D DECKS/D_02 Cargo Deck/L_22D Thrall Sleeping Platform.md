# L_22D Thrall Sleeping Platform
An elevated sleeping platform built directly above [[L_22A Thrall Common Room|L_22A]], its floor forming the ceiling of the common room below. One side shares the wall of the adjacent Deposit, while the remaining sides are enclosed by the vessel's thick organic bulkheads. The platform is open to the corridor, with no door or other means of separation.
Access is normally provided by an external ladder fixed beside the common room. During emergencies, however, occupants can leave the platform directly by dropping into the corridor below, allowing the thralls to assemble rapidly without bottlenecking at the ladder.
Sleeping accommodation consists of rows of simple metal bunk beds arranged with little regard for privacy or comfort. The platform serves only as a place of rest, reflecting the illithid view that thralls require no personal quarters beyond the minimum necessary to maintain their usefulness.
