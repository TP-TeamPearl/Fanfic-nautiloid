# PH_02 Arcane Transporter
The Arcanic Transporter is one of the few arcane mechanisms native to the nautiloid prior to launch. It predates the current mission and was retained by the vessel's previous illithid operators rather than replaced. Although its principles are only partially understood, it remains reliable and efficient for material handling. No attempt was made to reproduce the system or convert it to a psionic equivalent, likely due to resource constraints and the absence of a suitable integration framework.

The transporter consists of a paired rail assembly mounted along the ceiling, forming a fixed transit path between the Cargo Deck lift [[L_25 Arcane Transporter|L_25]] and the storage area known informally as the Deposit [[L_24A The Deposit|L_24A]]. Cargo crates are suspended between the rails, lifted vertically from the loading point, carried along the overhead track, and released at their destination.

The distance between the rails is fixed. Rather than substantially altering the original mechanism, the Banites standardised the fittings of their cargo containers to match its existing dimensions, ensuring stable alignment during transit. The system is limited to repeatable linear movement and cannot independently route cargo elsewhere.
At, the transporter is connected to a dedicated command board from which an illithid operator can initiate and regulate cargo movement along the rails. A standardised warning mark has been added to the floor beneath the loading zone, identifying the area that must remain clear while suspended cargo is being raised or lowered.
Crates travelling toward [[L_24A The Deposit|L_24A]] pass through a reinforced aperture in the iron bulkhead before reaching the unloading point. Human access through this passage is blocked by an iron door, while cargo can continue along the overhead rails. Workers inside the Deposit manually remove and stack arriving containers, keeping the final stage of the process independent of the transport mechanism.

The transporter can be physically observed from the Thrall Overseer's office [[L_18A Thrall Overseer's Office|L_18A]], including the loading area around the lift and cargo moving along the exposed section of rail. This provides functional oversight, but also serves a disciplinary purpose: workers remain aware that their activity may be observed without being able to determine when the overseeing illithid is actually watching.

The overhead configuration keeps suspended cargo above the principal circulation routes, reducing obstruction and physical strain during routine logistics. Its purpose is not primarily speed or throughput, but the reliable movement of heavy loads through a confined vessel without requiring workers to carry them along the corridor.
