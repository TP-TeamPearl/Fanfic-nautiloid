# L_24A The Deposit
The Deposit occupies the forward section of the lower deck, positioned just before the vessel’s tentacular structures.
Originally, the Deposit appears to have functioned as a transient staging area rather than a true storage hold. Scattered throughout the space are remnants of illithid machinery from previous campaigns—fractured organic frames, inert psionic housings, and tools whose original functions are no longer fully understood. Many of these devices are partially embedded in the surrounding architecture, having been abandoned rather than removed when their utility diminished. They are not entirely inert, but neither are they operational; they persist as residues of prior use, tolerated rather than curated.
In preparation for the current mission, the human crew repurposed the Deposit extensively. Standardized crates now dominate the space, stacked in uneven rows and secured with improvised restraints. These contain dry provisions, personal effects such as clothing and books, and a significant volume of technical material intended for later installation in laboratories and offices throughout the ship. As a result, the Deposit has become a layered environment: older illithid remnants buried behind and beneath newer human cargo, with neither fully displacing the other.
