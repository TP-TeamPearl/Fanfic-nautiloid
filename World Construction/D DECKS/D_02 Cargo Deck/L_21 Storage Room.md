# L_21 Storage Room
The original purpose of this compartment is difficult to determine. The human crew now uses it as an informal storage room for bulky equipment and miscellaneous belongings considered useful enough to bring aboard, but not important or personal enough to keep in their quarters.
Among the accumulated material are pieces of alien laboratory furniture, damaged equipment, and remnants recovered from previous expeditions undertaken by the nautiloid. Some may predate the current crew entirely. Access to the room is restricted by a psionic veil, making it somewhat more secure than its improvised function would suggest.
## Inventory:
- Hospital beds
- Shelves of refrigerated medications
- Damaged and obsolete equipment
