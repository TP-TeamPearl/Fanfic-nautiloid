# L_19 Unidentified Illithid Workstation
An open compartment distinguished by an unusual raised threshold. Rather than closing the entrance with a door, the lower portion of the wall extends across it as a retaining barrier, allowing the floor of the chamber to remain permanently flooded with a shallow layer of unidentified liquid.
At the centre stands a large machine pillar whose lower mechanisms remain partially immersed. The liquid may serve as a coolant, psionic conductor, or biological medium required by the apparatus, although its exact function has not been determined.
A specialised seat is connected directly to the pillar and positioned behind its bulk, providing a minimal degree of privacy despite the chamber's open entrance. The seat incorporates an aperture beneath the occupant and several interfaces corresponding to the dorsal spine. Its construction suggests that an illithid operator was expected to remain connected to the apparatus for exceptionally long periods without leaving the station.
Whatever process originally took place here appears to have required sustained physical and psionic integration between operator, machine, and vessel. 
## Inventory:
- Integrated illithid operator seat
- Dorsal interface connections
- Central machine pillar
- Shallow unidentified liquid
