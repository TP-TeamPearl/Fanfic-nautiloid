# L_24B The Corner
Within the Deposit, a concealed gathering space has been established by [[CH_04]] and [[CH_02]], informally referred to as the Corner. It is tucked between stacked crates and a shelf of secured wine bottles, partially shielded from the main transit paths. The space contains no permanent furnishings; crates are rearranged as needed to serve as seats or tables.

The Corner functions as a temporary refuge during low-activity periods. The two maintain a small lockbox filled with buttons, scraps of metal, and minor baubles, which they treat as currency during card games. The items have no material value, but the ritual of betting and winning provides structure and distraction. As the crew continues to explore the vessel, more curiosities are quietly added to the box.
## Inventory:
- 3 game cards
- "Treasure box" containing buttons, gold coins, and polished stones
- Wine shelf full bottles
- Wine goblets
- Wooden table and stools
