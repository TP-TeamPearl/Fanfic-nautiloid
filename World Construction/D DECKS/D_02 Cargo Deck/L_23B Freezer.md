# L_23B Freezer
The Neural Preservation Vault is a sealed auxiliary chamber integrated into the Illithid Kitchen [[L_22A Thrall Common Room|L_23A]]. Its function is to arrest biological decay and delay metabolic degradation when immediate consumption or secondary use is not possible.
Atmospheric composition remains breathable, but cold saturation occurs rapidly, inducing numbness, slowed motor function, and eventual unconsciousness. The chamber is monitored only for temperature stability and containment integrity.
Illithids, lack olfactory perception. A prepared brain may appear valid for consummation, while carrying compounds that prove harmful only upon ingestion.
[[CH_07]] has gradually appropriated part of the compartment for his own work, installing shelves for medications and experimental substances requiring low-temperature storage.
