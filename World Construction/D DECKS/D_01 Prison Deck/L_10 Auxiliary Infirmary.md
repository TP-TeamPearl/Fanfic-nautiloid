# L_10 Auxiliary Infirmary
A long rectangular chamber containing equipment associated with medical treatment, though its original purpose can no longer be determined with certainty. Unlike the vessel's principal infirmary, the room lacks the environmental controls and sanitary arrangements expected of a surgical facility. Whether it once served as an overflow treatment area, a recovery ward, or simply a repository for surplus equipment remains unknown.
The compartment contains an incongruous mixture of native illithid furnishings and human medical supplies introduced during the Banite refit. No personnel are assigned to the room, and the gradual biological deformation of the surrounding structure suggests it has remained unused since before the vessel's departure. Portions of the walls have begun to reclaim the space, obscuring where doorways and adjoining compartments once existed.
## Inventory:
- Metal shelving
- Wheeled hospital beds
- Examination table
- Illithid control panels
- Assorted medical supplies
- Surgical operating block
