# OI_03 Water reservoirs
Reservoirs in [[L_01 Water reservoirs Deposit|L_01]] are passively replenished by the vessel itself. Air entering through the large opening in the bridge is drawn through the vessel's living tissues, where moisture is extracted by the primary purification organ—the natural pools located within the Illithid Garden [[L_53 Garden Pools|L_53]] and behind the lift rail. 
During prolonged contact with water, the vessel's hull can also absorb and channel water directly into the reservoirs, although this process is considerably less efficient.

> [!NOTE] Crew concerns
> The human crew refuses to drink from it, fearing it may contain particles harmful to their metabolism, since the nautiloid is partly composed of alien flesh and metal of unknown origin. A human system is installed in [[L_48 Alien Garden# OH_03 Water reservoirs|L_48]]
