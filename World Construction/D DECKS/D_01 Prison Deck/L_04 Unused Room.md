# L_04 *Unused Room*
An abandoned compartment undergoing gradual biological reclamation. Much of the original architecture has deteriorated beneath layers of living tissue, leaving only fragments of the former floor exposed. The entrance has become noticeably constricted as the surrounding flesh slowly reforms the opening, indicating that the vessel has begun reabsorbing the chamber.
Although presently inaccessible for normal use, the compartment is not entirely isolated. Utility conduits and biological channels continue to pass through its walls, making it a potential location for maintenance work or future redevelopment should the vessel's needs change.
