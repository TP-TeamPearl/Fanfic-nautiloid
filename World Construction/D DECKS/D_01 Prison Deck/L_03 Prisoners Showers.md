# L_03 Prisoners Showers
A long communal washing chamber segmented by a row of mirrored floor squares, each designating an individual standing position. Prisoners bathe in the open under constant observation, with no partitions or enclosed spaces. The water supply is controlled from the Thrall Quartermaster's Office in [[L_18A Thrall Overseer's Office|L_18A]].
