# L_07 Intake Chamber Intake Chamber 
All abducted individuals are routed by default to this chamber upon arrival aboard the vessel. The chamber functions as a containment and staging area prior to disinfection and further processing.
Disinfection protocols are semi-automated through systems controlled from [[L_18A Thrall Overseer's Office|L_18A]] and remain inactive unless explicitly engaged. 
When the deck's resonance stone is active, direct supervision is minimal: a thrall provides shaving tools, and the captives, rendered docile, are expected to shave one another. Otherwise, an illithid must remain present to keep the captive subdued while the thrall shaves their head.

# Disinfection
- Source: The Illithiad
- Subject: Thralls, captives
Freshly claimed subjects undergo immediate processing by their illithid captors, which includes total body shaving, application of a de-bugging powder, and a medical evaluation. Ongoing sanitization ensures that infectious conditions do not compromise the rest of the collective's thralls.
