# L_02 Prison 
These individual cells are designated for prisoners awaiting processing—typically as food stock, thralls, or sleeper assets—following disinfection and initial assessment. The cells are configured for extended humanoid detention, indicating that one of the vessel’s operational objectives was to enter conflict zones to capture political prisoners...
