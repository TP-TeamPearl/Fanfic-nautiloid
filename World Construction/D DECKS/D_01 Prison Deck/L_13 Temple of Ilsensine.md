# Temple of Ilsensine
Little more than an alcove opening directly onto the corridor, this shrine is the vessel's sole space formally dedicated to Ilsensine. Its crude construction reflects the limited importance afforded to religious practice aboard the nautiloid: no chamber was sacrificed for its construction, nor were significant resources spent upon ornamentation.
A shallow basin occupies most of the recess. Its dark, conductive liquid is surrounded by irregular psionic sigils and Qualith inscriptions cut directly into the adjoining surfaces. 

*Rather than prayers or offerings, these markings describe relationships between concepts—associations, classifications, contradictions, sequences, and patterns accumulated and revised over time. Some overlap older inscriptions or connect ideas whose relationship is not immediately apparent.*

To nonillithids, prolonged exposure to the alcove is unpleasant. Thoughts become difficult to separate from associations, familiar concepts suggest unwanted connections, and concentration may produce the uncomfortable impression that one's mind is being categorized rather than observed.

*[[CI_01]] uses this place to direct its attention toward Ilsensine. Whether the shrine actually facilitates divine contact, merely prepares the worshipper's mind to receive it, or serves only as an elaborate instrument of concentration cannot be readily established.*
