# L_11 Unidentified Rooms
Several adjoining compartments whose original layout has become increasingly difficult to reconstruct. Biological reclamation is well advanced throughout this section of the vessel, with swollen walls gradually engulfing former doorways, alcoves, and built-in furnishings. The chambers appear to have been abandoned long before the current mission.
Fragments of illithid control panels and broken Qualith tablets remain partially exposed where the surrounding tissue has begun to digest them. Thin rivulets of conductive fluid seep from the walls, carrying a sharp metallic odour mixed with the acrid scent of decaying organic matter. The process appears deliberate rather than pathological, as though the vessel is reclaiming obsolete structures for future growth.
## Inventory:
- Partially absorbed Qualith tablets
- Remnants of illithid control panels
- Exposed conduit veins
