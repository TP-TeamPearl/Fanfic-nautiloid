# L_09 Circular Chamber
A circular chamber arranged around a central transformation pod. Strange illithid seats are distributed evenly along the perimeter, each integrated into the vessel's psionic network.
At the centre of the room rests a specialised transformation pod capable of initiating the final stage of ceremorphosis through commands issued from an adjacent console. 
The precise purpose of maintaining multiple observer stations is uncertain. They may permit collective supervision, synchronise several minds during exceptional procedures, or simply provide redundant access to the vessel's shared consciousness.
