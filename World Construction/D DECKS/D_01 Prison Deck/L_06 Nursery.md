## Tadpole
- Source: Lords of Madness
In its natural life cycle, the mind flayer is amphibious. Adults reproduce hermaphroditically only a few times, laying roughly a thousand eggs in a specially prepared briny pool. The eggs hatch after about a month. Tadpoles remain in the nursery for roughly ten years, feeding on a mixture of brain tissue and other organs. They grow to around three inches but remain only marginally more intelligent than animals.
An elder brain dwells in the same pool and feeds on the tadpoles. Only a tiny fraction survive the decade, and these survivors are regarded as the strongest and most cunning specimens, earning the right to undergo ceremorphosis.
