# FI_05 Resonance Stone Pillar
Biomechanical infrastructure containing **5 Resonance Stone slots**. The pillar becomes active when at least one slot is occupied and functions as a range amplifier, allowing the resonance produced by the inserted stones to affect a larger area. The effect does not influence illithids.
*The pillar can modulate the intensity and pulse of the transmitted resonance, producing different emotional effects depending on the stones installed.*

**Operational Use:** 
Illithid crews ordinarily activate the Pillar during missions as an inexpensive first step in breaking the resistance of eventual captives, either preparing them for interrogation or simply keeping them quiet. The unusual presence of a human crew on the Prison Deck forced the illithids to operate without this routine measure, exposing them to a challenge they had rarely needed to account for.

# OI_09 Resonance Stone
A mineral or crystal that continuously radiates a specific emotion within a limited area. Commonly used by illithids to influence mood and control captives.

## Resonance Stone
- Source: Lords of Madness
They function as emotion transmitters: each stone holds a particular emotion and constantly radiates it within a limited area, usually around 30 feet. Stronger stones affect larger spaces, while tiny ones may need direct contact to be felt. A stone is always active. The closer someone gets, the stronger the emotion becomes, with direct contact producing the greatest intensity.

**Used for control:** Stones emitting helplessness and hopelessness are often used to pacify newly captured prisoners until they can be enthralled. In holding pits, stones producing resignation or contentment can discourage noise and violence. During interrogation, stones carrying emotions such as terror, despair, or surrender can make captives easier to control.

*Illithids use them casually in homes (feelings of deep self-satisfaction, pride, personal superiority,  elation, satiation, confidence, …) and public spaces (feelings of responsibility, duty, loyalty, or racial superiority), choosing emotions such as .. They can also use combinations of stones to shape an experience.

They can be made from almost any mineral, with crystal, quartz, marble, and gemstones being common.
