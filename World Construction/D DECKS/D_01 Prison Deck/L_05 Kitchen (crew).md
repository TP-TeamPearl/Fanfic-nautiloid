# L_05 Kitchen (crew)
A corridor corner refitted into a communal dining area by and for the humanoid crew. Unlike the native compartments of the nautiloid, the place has been furnished with practical amenities and improved lighting.
The surrounding place is generally quiet. Most prisoner activity is confined to the deck above, while the cells on this level are unoccupied, allowing the engineering and scientific personnel to take their meals with a degree of privacy and minimal illithid presence.
## Inventory:
- Wine shelf
- Long stone table
- Wooden crates arranged as benches
- Potted tree
- Sink
