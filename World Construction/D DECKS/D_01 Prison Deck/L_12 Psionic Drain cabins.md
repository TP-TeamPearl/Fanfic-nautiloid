# L_12 Psionic Drain cabins
These chambers combine enforced rest with regulated extraction. A reclining stone chair—lacking the cultivated moss that cushions personal sleeping chambers—may be lowered into nutrient water or held above the bath's surface depending on the subject’s preference. From the ceiling, a Psionic Siphon slowly draws psionic output from the occupant, routing it directly to the ship’s reservoirs [[L_08B Psionic Corner|L_08B]].

A curved recess beside the bath provides space for personal effects during periods of rest.
The number of cabins initially mirrored the birthing pods in [[L_06 Nursery |L_06]]. They are primarily assigned to newly born illithids, who lack private quarters until reintegration into a colony. During this interim period, their primary function is energetic contribution toward sustaining active vessel systems and crew.
## Inventory:

- A Psionic Siphon
