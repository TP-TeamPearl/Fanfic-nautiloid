# OI_04 Psionic Reservoir
An enlarged variant of the malleable psionic gobbet—illithid mucus bound within a transparent membrane—commonly worn as a neck reservoir. These units store psionic energy as condensed potential, usable by vessel defense systems or redirected to augment individual illithids.

Recharging a reservoir requires prolonged concentration and is typically conducted during enforced rest cycles.

Stored energy is allocated according to priority: 
- hull weaponry
- core machinery redundancy
- tactical redistribution during high-risk states

With the crew reduced, [[CI_05]] is solely allocated to provide its psionic energy. 
*The current travel conditions make immediate depletion through defensive systems unlikely, allowing the reservoirs to build to a sufficient level before the vessel reaches more dangerous territory.*
