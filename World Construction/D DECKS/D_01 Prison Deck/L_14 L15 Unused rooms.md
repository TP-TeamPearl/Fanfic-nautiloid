# L_14 L15 Unused rooms
Two small compartments crudely converted by the Banites into additional offices and sleeping quarters. Their furnishings are minimal, with stone tables repurposed as beds and little effort made beyond rendering the rooms functional for humanoid occupants.
The spaces were prepared for members no longer available by the departure, leaving both rooms without permanent occupants. 
The illithid crew considers the surrounding section sensitive and objects to unnecessary humanoid presence there. As a result, both rooms have fallen out of attribution.
## Inventory:
- Stone tables repurposed as beds
- Basic office furniture
