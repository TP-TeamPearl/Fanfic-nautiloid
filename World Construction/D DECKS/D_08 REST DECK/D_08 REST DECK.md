[[L_82 CI_03's Rest Room]]

The Rest Deck occupies the highest curvature of the nautiloid's shell, nestled within the convex crown of the vessel. The thrum of the engines is almost completely muted here.

A broad central corridor runs through the deck. Along its length stands a high stone shelf, carved seamlessly from the surrounding structure and interrupted only where the passage bends toward the individual quarters.
Illithid accesses are positioned at either end of the corridor, providing direct passage to the lower decks through openings in the floor.
The private quarters are arranged in mirrored pairs along the corridor. One room opens directly toward the passage; its counterpart is reversed, with its entrance reached only after following the corridor and turning ninety degrees into the room's side.

This reversed arrangement leaves the corridor space in front of the room as a semi-private extension of the quarters. A small work area can occupy the corner, while the inner face of the stone shelf provides additional usable space. The individual chamber remains separated from the passage by a psionic veil.
The arrangement creates a quiet, sheltered open-space area for each occupant, with greater room than the sleeping chamber itself. Being positioned toward the interior of the crown also provides some protection from damage to the outer shell and prow.

The atmosphere is deliberately calm and detached from the activity of the lower vessel.
