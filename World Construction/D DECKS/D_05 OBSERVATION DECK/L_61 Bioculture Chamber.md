# L_61 Bioculture Chamber
Space dedicated to microbial cultures and biological sample observation. Materials collected during analysis in Lab [[L_65 Necrotic Lab|L_65]] are transferred here for long-term monitoring, growth experiments, and biochemical decomposition studies.
Opposite the cryogenic rack stand a series of tall glass-fronted shelves embedded into the wall. The shelves store both illithid research materials and basic human medical supplies—bandages, antiseptic compounds, stabilizing tonics, and other emergency treatments maintained for the non-illithid crew.
A rectangular service opening has been built into one wall, forming a simple exchange hatch between the chamber and the adjoining passage. The opening allows an illithid to approach and request materials without entering the area itself.

# PI_08 Substrate Spectrometer
Analytical device in [[L_61 Bioculture Chamber|L_61]] used to examine biological and chemical samples recovered aboard the vessel.

The Substrate Spectrometer stands as a tall, skeletal instrument anchored directly into the deck plating. Its base is surrounded by thick, root-like conduits resembling blood vessels. The main body rises into a rigid frame supporting a tiered rack of slender sample pipettes and glass-like tubes, each held in narrow clamps of pale chitin. Samples are usually collected in these pipettes before being placed into the rack for examination.

Above the rack hangs the analysis head, a curved mechanical-organic structure resembling the nozzle of an elaborate brewing machine. From its underside emerges flexible sensory organ—a narrow, muscular “tongues” capable of extending downward to meet the sample pipettes individually.

When activated, the probe lowers and presses against the pipette tip. Capillary filaments emerge along its surface, drawing microscopic traces of the sample while measuring weight, temperature, density, opacity, and movement patterns within the liquid.
At the base of the apparatus, the vascular tendrils occasionally rise to touch the lower ends of the pipettes directly, acting as secondary receptors for chemical composition and psionic resonance. These contact points pulse faintly as the device interprets molecular and energetic signatures.

Information gathered by the system is stored in an internal qualith memory lattice embedded within the central column.
Recorded data can be retrieved through qualith interface plates mounted along the device's outer frame [[PI_03 Logger|Logger]].

## OI_01 Sample Manipulation Rail
The desk beneath the [[# PI_08 Substrate Spectrometer|Substrate Spectrometer]] is equipped with a circular reagent rail. This rotating track contains several glass reservoirs and sealed containers mounted in articulated clamps.

Each container connects to the central sample rack through narrow valves controlled psionically by the seated operator. When activated, the clamps shift along the rail and release measured droplets or vapors into selected pipettes. The process occurs with slow mechanical precision, guided entirely by mental commands rather than manual handling.
The rail allows the illithid to introduce catalysts, toxins, stabilizing fluids, or experimental compounds into the samples while remaining seated in the interface.

# OI_11 Tall Glass Shelves
The shelves hold a mixture of containers: stoppered glass vials, sealed sample tubes, reagent bottles, and occasional alien materials stored in translucent membranes or resin capsules.

*Because the shelving extends high along the wall, a sliding ladder mounted on a rail allows human technicians to reach the upper tiers. Illithids rarely use this mechanism, typically retrieving items through levitation.*
