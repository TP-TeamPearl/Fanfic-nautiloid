The Iron Passage [[L_58A Iron Passage|L_58A]] forms the principal route from the bridge deck toward the vessel's upper working and habitation spaces. It begins beyond the open expanse surrounding the central lift and continues toward the craft workshops, command room, and illithid resting chambers.

The bridge-facing entrance is deliberately broad and unobstructed. A long iron shutter is mounted within the surrounding structure and can be lowered to seal the entire passage during an attack, isolating the upper sections of the vessel from the bridge. The barrier was designed as a defensive measure rather than a routine door and is normally kept raised.
The passage was originally intended for movement by illithids alone, as the upper sections of the vessel being of little concern to thralls. The human crew's occupation of the craft workshops and adjacent offices required a modification to the lift system, allowing it to reach this otherwise restricted level.
