# L_64 Mind-Archive Room
A cramped archival chamber protected by a psionic veil. High shelves hold preserved humanoid brains in containment jars.
A prototype brain-reading apparatus, adapted from technology used in the nautiloid colonies, allows humans to question the preserved minds without direct psionic communion. The chamber was not designed for human access: the upper shelves cannot be reached without a scale, and the atmosphere of the illithid laboratory makes the room widely avoided.

# PH_03 Mind Archive Interface
Allows a [[OI_08 Brain Jar# OI_08 Brain Jar|Brain Jar]] (bottled in [[L_23A Illithid Kitchen|23A]]) to be inserted into the apparatus and the preserved mind to be questioned through a vocal interface.
The machine does not restore the subject or provide complete access to its memories. It stimulates residual neural activity and produces fragmented responses shaped by memory, personality, emotional fixation, and the condition of the brain at preservation.
Useful information must be elicited through dialogue. Responses may be incomplete, distorted, repetitive, or dominated by the subject's final experiences.
Emotional agitation can also intensify residual psionic activity. Illithids may therefore value particularly fearful, guilty, desperate, or obsessive subjects for reasons beyond the information they contain.
The archive can preserve technical knowledge, historical and planar information, personal experience, and other memories deemed useful by the crew.
