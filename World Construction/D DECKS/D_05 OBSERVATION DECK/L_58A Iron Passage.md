# FH_00 IRON SHUTTER

- Crisis: Emergency Deployment. In emergency situations, an iron shutter seals off the observation deck, preventing any access regardless of clearance level.

Pipes connected to the laboratory [[L_65 Necrotic Lab|L_65]] run along the passage and terminate near the barrier. In an emergency, the system can discharge chemical or biological agents into the sealed approach, discouraging attackers from attempting to breach or dismantle the shutter.
Constructed from interlocking metal blades, this internal shutter is deployed during attacks or breaches to isolate the lift and upper decks.

**Additional Functionality:**
A gas release valve—operated from the laboratory [[L_65 Necrotic Lab|L_65]]—can flood the area beyond the shutter with a non-lethal or psionically disorienting gas. This gas disperses through slits in the metal.

*Gas deployment introduces tactical CD. Suggest implementing sensor-linked triggers that detect aggression/unauthorized mass movement before activation.*
