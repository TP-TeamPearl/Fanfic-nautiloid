# L_59 Unresolved Growth
A section of the vessel's structure near the lift has developed into something irregular that cannot be satisfactorily identified. Its surface appears to fold into itself without consistent depth or orientation, producing edges and recesses that seem to shift when viewed from different angles and operating in a non-euclidean geometry.
The human crew generally avoids approaching or looking directly at it.

*To preserve the Lovecraftian vibe, the horror should exist entirely in observable consequence, only in what happens to people who interact with the Growth.*
