# L_57 Secretariat
A secluded chamber protected by a persistent psionic veil. The interior retains entirely illithid furnishings and equipment. The room serves as the vessel's administrative and imprinting station, where crew schedules, ranks, and authorized access are encoded into psionic seals before departure or following reassignment.
The room is normally attended by illithid personnel responsible for security and psionic craftsmanship. Human crew members enter only when required for imprinting, calibration, or other authorized procedures.

# PI_01 Clocquartz
Central system regulating the sleep-wake cycles using a crystal quartz and psionic imprint tattoo. It alerts each crew member with a specific frequency tailored to their [[Planning|daily schedule]].

The device takes the form of a semi-organic pillar embedded in the vessel's upper spine, shaped like an elongated, stylized hourglass. Psionic impulses circulate through its structure continuously, resembling blood moving through a living organ.
The Clocquartz divides the crew's routine into recurring periods of sleep, recovery, personal time, and duty. Individual schedules are offset so that critical functions remain continuously staffed.

Each crew member bears a psionic seal calibrated to the vessel's schedule and authority structure. The practice derives from established illithid use of psionic markings and was extended to the human crew under Banite administration. The seals identify their bearers and can grant access to designated areas or systems according to rank and assignment. 
*To the humans, the markings resemble the brands carried by thralls elsewhere aboard the vessel; few initially realize that comparable seals are worn by the illithids themselves (but for daily schedule only).*

# PI_04 Psionic Circuitry Printer
A precision illithid device used to imprint prepared psionic circuitry onto objects or living creatures.

The primary imprinting instrument is a movable, articulated applicator resembling an elongated tattoo stylus. Its head contains several independently adjustable contact elements, allowing the device to maintain precise contact with irregular surfaces and difficult-to-reach areas. Multiple fine rollers or movable points can reposition themselves during operation, following the programmed geometry of the seal rather than relying on the target presenting a flat surface.

When used on living tissue, the resulting mark may resemble a tattoo to the untrained observer, but its visible pattern is only the external anchor for circuitry extending into the underlying tissue.
Each completed imprint is registered by the local Logger. The record includes the blueprint used, the target designation, and the calibration parameters applied during printing, allowing the system to distinguish between an intentionally reproduced seal and an unauthorized or manually altered one.

--- 

Rather than requiring the seal to be individually constructed from scratch, our printer receives a defined psionic blueprint and reproduces its circuitry with consistent geometry and function across multiple targets.  ^FicPrint

*The machine is normally activated through a dedicated physical authorization key inserted into a receiver built into its housing. This mechanism predates the vessel's current psionic security infrastructure and remains available as an emergency fallback. In circumstances where psionic systems must be suppressed or minimized, such as during operations intended to avoid Gith detection, the machine can therefore still be operated manually by an authorized holder.
Under ordinary conditions, the room's psionic veil and associated security measures make the physical key largely redundant.*

# FI_04C
The archive of the Clocquartz [[L_57 Secretariat# FI_04C|FI_04C]] retains the identity associated with each imprint, the selected seal configuration, authorization state, and relevant Clocquartz calibration data. It therefore provides a persistent record of crew designation and synchronization settings even when the vessel's wider psionic communications are unavailable.
