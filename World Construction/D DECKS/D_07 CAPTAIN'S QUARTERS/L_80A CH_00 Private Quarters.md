# L_80A CH_00 Private Quarters
A room mirroring [[L_79A Captain's Rest room|L_79A]].
A spacious and luxurious room (by illithid standards), originally assigned to the vessel's co-pilot. A broad strip along the room contains a self-cleaning pool intended for relaxation, while a wall-mounted illithid recliner sits near the ceiling above a planted floor area, accessible by levitation.

The room was later adapted for Captain [[CH_00]]. A staircase leads to a small mezzanine containing his bedroom, [[L_80B Ch_00 Rest Room|L_80B]].
Human-made Deck Status Panels display the vessel's current situation. Colored indicators identify requests awaiting attention from the various decks, providing a simple local overview of ship activity.
