# L_81 CI_01's Rest Room
A narrow room enclosed between the larger private quarters of the captain and co-pilot. Its entrance is not within the command room itself, but through an illithid access near the ceiling of the adjoining corridor.
The room contains an illithid bed and equipment dedicated to the study of the sky and the wider multiverse. Material-plane instruments are kept alongside illithid tools, suggesting a practical interest in comparing unfamiliar methods of navigation and observation.
## Inventory:
- Illithid bed
- Boxes of parchment
- Compass and other material-plane instruments
- Astronomical and navigational tools
- Illithid study equipment
