# L_75 The Helm
The helm is designed for two illithid pilots seated side by side. One pilot's position has been fitted with a human seat and an added rotating mechanism, allowing it to be repositioned for [[CH_00]]'s role as captain.
In front of the captain, several illithid control panels have been bridged with arcane human instrumentation. Modulators, converters, and other removable components must be stripped or rearranged when an illithid retakes the seat before the original controls can be restored.
A separate rail runs between the two pilot seats, carrying a compact control unit within [[CH_00]]'s immediate reach. Rather than reproducing the illithid interface, it provides crude human equivalents: levers, selectors, switches, and other controls intended to translate direct mechanical or arcane inputs into commands for the vessel. Much of this system remains incomplete or barely functional.

- VISUAL DISPLAYS: Three screens are positioned directly before the pilots. With no windows or external view, they provide the helm with an artificial visual feed. The central display can relay live or near-live transmissions from [[CH_09 Gortash|Gortash]] when he addresses the vessel's crew. When no transmission is active, the screens may instead display a visual feed from the Bridge Deck or relay radar and other sensor information from the Communication Room.

# PH_04 SPELLJAMMING HELM 
In [[L_75 The Helm|L_75]], the function of this ornate chair is to propel and maneuver a ship on which it has been installed through space and air. 
The sensation of being attuned to a spelljamming helm is akin to the pins-and-needles effect one experiences after one's arm or leg falls asleep, but not as painful. 
While attuned to a spelljamming helm and sitting in it, you gain the following abilities for as long as you maintain concentration (as if concentrating on a spell): 

• You can use the spelljamming helm to move the ship through space and air (not water for the nautiloid) up to the ship's speed. 
• You can steer the vessel, albeit in a somewhat clumsy fashion, in much the way that a rudder or oars can be used to maneuver a seafaring ship.

# ASTRAL SEA NAVIGATION
- Source: Spelljammer Adventures in Space
Maps are unnecessary for navigating the Astral Sea. Creatures there possess an innate sense of direction that allows them to reach a destination simply by focusing on it mentally. Once they do, they perceive the most direct path to any location within the Astral Sea or Wildspace, such as a githyanki outpost, Hestavar, or Realmspace.

This awareness provides direction, not information about the journey. It does not indicate the route's safety, distance, or hazards; those factors are determined by the DM.
