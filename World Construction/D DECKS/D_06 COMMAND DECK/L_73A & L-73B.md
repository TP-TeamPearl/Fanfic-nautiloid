# L_73A & L-73B
Two mirrored technical chambers occupy either side of the corridor leading toward [[L_75 The Helm|L_75]]. Their interiors are separated from the corridor by heavy iron grilles and secured by iron doors.
Each chamber contains a tall cylindrical mechanism composed almost entirely of conventional mechanical components rather than the vessel's usual biomechanical structures. The cylinder is divided into multiple rotating discs, each turning independently and producing a distinct, persistent mechanical tone. The combined sound varies as the discs change speed or alignment. Hammering can occasionally be heard from within the mechanism. Air is also driven forcefully through a narrow passage behind one of the lateral grilles, producing a noticeable pressure along the corridor.

Opposite each machine, a series of control panels provides access to its operating systems. Unlike much of the vessel's infrastructure, the associated cables are carefully routed and organized.
