# L_69 Strategic Cartography Chamber
A chamber originally dedicated to strategic cartography and long-range conquest planning. Its walls are fitted with recessed projection surfaces capable of displaying star fields, territorial boundaries, navigational routes, and other large-scale strategic information.
At the centre of the chamber, a suspended three-dimensional projection system can render celestial bodies, settlements, orbital paths, and areas of interest as an interactive spatial model. The resulting displays give the room an unusual sense of depth, with projected light and moving constellations occupying the walls and air around the occupants.

# Phase_2: 
After the resonance stone was activated in the lower decks by [[CI_04]], the chamber was repurposed by the human crew as a meeting and lounge space. Materials previously stored here were moved into the surrounding corridor, and human furniture and decorations were introduced into the room, substantially altering its original atmosphere.
