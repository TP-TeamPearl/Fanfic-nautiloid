# L_66 Eating corner (Illithid)
Positioned near the command room in the large open space, it allows the pilots to feed without remaining absent from their stations for long. During these intervals, routine navigation is left to the nautiloid's autonomous functions and supervised by the human command crew.
To preserve the limited floor space during ordinary operations, the chamber's ritual altar is normally retracted against the ceiling.
## Inventory:
- Ritualistic altar ceiling-mounted.
