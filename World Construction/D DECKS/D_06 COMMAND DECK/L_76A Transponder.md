# PI_15 Transponder
A large planar transit apparatus integrated into the vessel's infrastructure and composed of several articulated organic elements. Its upper tentacles rise independently from the main housing, seeking outward as if growing toward an unseen source.

To initiate a transit, two selected tendrils must be brought together at their extremities, where they establish a strong energetic connection. The connected limbs are then drawn apart and held under tension like the string of a bow. Releasing them discharges the accumulated energy and forces the vessel across planar boundaries.

The process is neither instantaneous nor easily repeatable. Each transit consumes substantial stored energy and specialized components, while the transponder requires an extended regeneration and recalibration period before another shift can be attempted. The vessel therefore cannot use planar travel as a routine means of escape or maneuver.

## **CHECK! CRISIS
A planar transition also temporarily removes the vessel from the Elder Brain's normal sphere of influence. 

Illithids aboard the vessel are consequently able to recover a measure of independent thought while outside its reach. Returning to an illithid colony is therefore not an inherently desirable destination for a crew that has retained its autonomy under human administration, particularly when doing so would place them once again beneath collective control.
The apparatus is consequently treated as a strategic resource rather than an ordinary navigation device. Its use requires deliberate authorization and is generally reserved for exceptional circumstances.

### Security - I see at least two options:

1. **The Hivemind lowered security among the illithids.**  
    This would explain why there is effectively no security lock preventing a newly tadpoled avatar from using the ship's systems—unlike Shadowheart's pod, which requires some form of activation. The hivemind may have granted all connected illithids a baseline level of access.
2. **Security was re-enforced when the githyanki were detected boarding the ship.**  
    Because of their powerful psionic abilities, the gith might have been considered capable of hijacking or manipulating illithid tech. In this scenario, the Emperor's behavior outside the hivemind could also have caused him to lose access to critical security devices. It would therefore have had to bypass or break the security itself.
