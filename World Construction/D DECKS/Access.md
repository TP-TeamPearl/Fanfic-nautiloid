# AI_02 PSIONIC VEIL
Access is strictly dependent on tattoo verification ([[TI_03 Psionic seals# TI_03 Psionic seals|seals]]). Passage through veiled access points is possible for prisoners only when an illithid escort actively suppresses the field for the duration of transit.
![[AI_02.jpg|243]]

A translucent psionic field used as a doorway or barrier. Veils are also placed across ramps and vertical passages, particularly on the cargo deck, to prevent unauthorized jumping or climbing toward restricted areas.

Humans without an authorized seal develop a frequency-based headache when approaching the field. Forcing passage can cause lethal neurological damage.
- Gith might be resistant. 
- Illithids remain unaffected.

**Features:** An internal override module located in [[L_18A Thrall Overseer's Office|L_18A]] and [[L_41 SECURITY Room|L_41]] allows security or command personnel to temporarily suspend tattoo-based authorization checks. When this override is active, unauthorized individuals may be moved through restricted zones without illithid escort. Such movement is recorded but flagged as unidentified if no valid psionic signature is detected.

# AH_01 Lift
A wide, steel-framed platform mounted on mechanical rails runs along the spinal axis of the nautiloid. It is operated through a junction box linked to both psionic relays and physical dials.
Primarily used by thralls and human crew—since illithids utilize their own ceiling-access shafts between decks—the lift facilitates the movement of heavy cargo, supply crates, and medical stretchers.

The lift descends from the [[D_04 BRIDGE DECK|Bridge Deck]]  to the [[D_01 PRISON DECK|Prison Deck]], where it interfaces with the overhead rail system. Upon arrival, only minimal lateral displacement of the rail carriage is required to align the suspended cargo with the receiving zone.
