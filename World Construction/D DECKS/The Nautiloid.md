# The Nautiloid
A nautiloid is a living warship, part organic being and part engineered vessel, designed for interdimensional conquest. Having passed through countless planes and eras, it carries layers of machinery that even its illithid caretakers no longer fully understand. Many systems have been hastily bridged or jury-rigged over successive campaigns, leaving fragile or deprecated connections and unpredictable functions among its secondary systems.

# Our Nautiloid
The vessel itself is in a state of constant evolution. Unused chambers are gradually reclaimed as metabolic dead zones, degenerating until they are absorbed by walls of living flesh, while new spaces may form in response to damage, stress, repair, or changing needs.

**Proprioception**
The nautiloid cannot communicate thoughts or images—it can only convey states. This communication resembles bodily sensation more than language: imbalance, pressure, congestion, tension, … 

These sensations can be differentiated through intensity, pulse, or shifts in perceived tone—something analogous to colours without actual visual information. 

The vessel does not see its surroundings but it has a crude spatial awareness through changes in gravity, pressure, motion, and the relative position of sufficiently perceptible objects. This allows it to perform simple autonomous corrections, such as lowering or gaining altitude, stabilising itself or avoiding an immediately perceived obstruction. However, it cannot reliably distinguish between an immediately interesting stimulus and an operationally relevant one.

The vessel is a living organism whose sensory priorities do not naturally coincide with those of its occupants. It possesses an instinctive tendency to investigate its surroundings with its tendrils rather than remain strictly focused on its established trajectory. This behaviour can accumulate minor deviations, cause unnecessary exposure to potential threats, and, most importantly, waste time.
# History of This Nautiloid

Many of the technologies accumulated aboard this particular vessel have already been bridged, deciphered, and readjusted over its service life. The ship appears to have been maintained primarily by illithids specialised in warfare and ballistics rather than artisans or arcane researchers. This may explain why several older arcane systems were preserved and adapted instead of being dismantled and replaced with cleaner and more sophisticated psionic devices.
The nautiloid assigned to this voyage is not so much a deviation from illithid doctrine as a controlled contamination of it. The presence of a birthing chamber containing dozens of pods, together with its elongated, predatory silhouette, suggests that it was built for aggressive abduction campaigns, probably during wars fought far from any established colony. Fortunately for its present operators, it remains fully armed.

Its internal anatomy remains mainly illithid vessel, but much of the interior has been refitted to sustain a human crew of engineers and scientists operating under Gortash's authority. Low ceilings, constricted corridors, and organic bulkheads have largely been left untouched, less from necessity than indifference.

One significant addition is artificial lighting. Illithids can see perfectly well in darkness and traditionally populate their vessels with thralls suited to the same conditions. The Banites therefore had to install a lighting network throughout the decks occupied by the human crew, adapting it to living walls and infrastructure never intended to accommodate one.

**Mission priority**
The vessel now serves as a mobile test bed for prototype machinery intended to bridge native illithid systems with interfaces accessible to tadpoled humanoid operators to reduce the number of true illithids required to operate a nautiloid.

This nautiloid is designated as a secondary asset, travelling in support of the primary retrieval flotilla rather than alongside its most exposed vessels. Its routes favour safer aerial corridors and delayed vectors, allowing it to remain available as backup support without unnecessarily exposing the prototypes aboard.

This reduces its expected risk, but Gith interception and planar hazards remain credible threats. The crew therefore maintains a constant state of operational readiness.
The vessel is not intended to make first contact with the Artifact or seize it independently. The prototype machinery aboard represents assets that [[CH_09 Gortash]] cannot easily reproduce if the ship is lost.
