# Sanitary Receptacle Unit 
As the nautiloid travels in silence, its waste evacuation system operates on a delayed cycle. Waste products are stored within a dedicated containment pouch, which accumulates necrotic vessel tissue, excess mucus, and any other liquid byproducts discharged aboard.

The container's fill level and composition (including toxicity percentage and organic density) are continuously relayed to the pilot's console [[L_75 The Helm|L_75]]. The pilot determines the appropriate moment for waste ejection. 
In the event of critical overcapacity, the vessel initiates disposal autonomously.
