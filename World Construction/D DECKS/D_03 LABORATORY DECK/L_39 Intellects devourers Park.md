# L_39 Intellects devourers Park
Intellect devourers are kept in individual cells arranged along the walls and extending upward toward the ceiling. While confined, their cognitive activity is deliberately reduced. They are awakened primarily for feeding, occasional movement, conditioning, or deployment during combat.
A long open area occupies the center of the room. When released from their cells, the intellect devourers gather and move through this space, allowing them to exercise, feed, and relieve themselves before being returned to confinement.
The central floor is designed for regular washing and drainage. Waste and other organic residue can be flushed toward the vessel's waste system, allowing the area to be cleaned after the creatures have been returned to their cells.

*Routine care is performed by thralls and do not normally require direct [[CI_04|illithid]] supervision.*
