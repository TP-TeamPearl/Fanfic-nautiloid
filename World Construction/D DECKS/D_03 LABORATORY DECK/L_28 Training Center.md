# L_28 Training Center
The room is designed to train humanoids—likely thralls and prisoners.
Target panels are embedded at variable heights along the perimeter—circular depressions designed to receive projectile strikes (or focused psionic discharge).
Along the central axis of the chamber stand several wooden training forms, roughly humanoid in shape, mounted on weighted bases to absorb force without toppling.

# OI_12 Sword rack
Illithids construct psychic weapons, generally longswords. They possess attack and damage bonuses. Wielders of higher Intelligence can also catalyze cumulative effects embedded in the psionic circuitry of the blade.
