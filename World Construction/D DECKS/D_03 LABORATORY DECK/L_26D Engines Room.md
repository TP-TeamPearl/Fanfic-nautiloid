# L_26D Engines Room
The Engines Room contains the mechanical systems responsible for sustaining the vessel's operation beyond its living tissues. While the nautiloid's flesh provides many of its own biological functions, the mechanical systems housed here provide additional energy, regulation, cooling, and structural support.
The installation occupies two vertically connected levels. Its primary machinery is located on Deck 3, while part of the system extends through the floor into a service chamber on Deck 2 [[L_26A Engines Room (Lower)|L_26A]].
Four large horizontal cylinders occupy the center of the room. Arranged as a square, they remain separated enough to permit an illithid to walk between them for inspection and maintenance.
The cylinders continuously reject moderately warm air through thick ventilation grids built into the surrounding walls. 
Adjacent to the cylinders is a second square formation consisting of four tall server-like blocks. These assemblies descend through the floor into the lower level and form the vertical portion of the engine system. 
Under normal operating conditions, the machinery remains submerged, allowing the surrounding water to act as part of its cooling and regulation system.
