# L_29 Human Crew Sanitary
L_29 is the primary sanitary facility intended for the human crew. The installation follows familiar humanoid sanitary arrangements rather than the thrall facilities found in the lower decks.
The room is divided into two aligned sections. Individual toilet compartments occupy one side, while separate shower compartments occupy the other. Each facility is enclosed sufficiently to provide privacy during use.
The installation is connected to the vessel's water and waste-management systems [[D_00 Sanitary Receptacle Unit]] through conventional human plumbing integrated into the surrounding biological structure.
A false ceiling conceals a shallow service space above the sanitary facilities, leading to [[L_37A Old Deposit|L_37A]]. Access is difficult, and the passage is not easily visible.
