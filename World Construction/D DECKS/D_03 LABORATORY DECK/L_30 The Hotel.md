# L_30 The Hotel
The Hotel is an informal human designation for a neglected residential section of the nautiloid. The area consists of two mirrored rows of small individual rooms arranged along a narrow central corridor guarded by a psionic veil. 
Evidence suggests that the section was once used as a low-scale work area for humanoid prisoners. The work performed there appears to have been concerned with maintaining the basic material needs of the vessel's humanoid population. Possible tasks included the repair and manufacture of simple clothing, gloves, belts, pouches, bedding, harnesses, fabric covers, and other textile or leather goods.
## Inventory:
- (Nothing magical) Few broken human tools, partial machines, mechanical components, lengths of wire and conduit, fasteners, fragments of treated leather and damaged containers.

> [!NOTE] 
> Material present in the Hotel does not appears in the vessel's official inventories!
