# L_32A The infirmary
Originally designed to support a team of illithids performing surgery, enthrallment and other biological alterations, the infirmary has been refitted to serve as the vessel's principal medical facility, where injuries are treated and routine healthcare is provided. Yet, original installations have been largely preserved.
The infirmary is operated jointly by [[CH_07]] and [[CH_06]].

*[[CH_07]] arranged for the equipment and materials he considered essential to be transferred from his residence before departure, allowing him to re-establish a familiar working environment aboard the vessel. Once assigned to [[L_32A The infirmary|L_32A]], he also tries to make use of the original illithid equipment.*
