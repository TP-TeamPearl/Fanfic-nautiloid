# L_33A Aqua-Lab
This unusually long compartment creates the impression of being narrower than it truly is. 
Along the left-hand wall extends a continuous aquarium, illuminated only by colonies of bioluminescent organisms clinging to submerged rock formations. Among them dwell pale, eel-like abyssal predators that spend most of their time concealed within the darkness. 
The opposite wall is occupied almost entirely by towering translucent reservoirs that rise nearly to the ceiling. They are large enough to accommodate humanoid-sized occupants. Between the reservoirs are native Qualith control panels whose functions have not been deciphered.
A thin trail of corrosive residue crosses the floor, leading towards a large iron door at the far end of the chamber [[L_33B Sealed Room|L_33B]].
