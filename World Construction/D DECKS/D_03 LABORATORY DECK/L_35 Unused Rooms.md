# L_35 Unused Rooms
A pair of small, deteriorating rooms occupy the opposite side of [[L_26D Engines Room|L_26D]] Engines Room. Both appear to have fallen out of use long ago, and their original purpose is unknown. 
Access remains difficult and the route is sufficiently inconvenient that most crew members have little reason to investigate it. The space is therefore unlikely to attract attention during ordinary activity aboard the vessel.
[[L_35 Unused Rooms|L_35A]] The entrance has partially distorted into the surrounding structure. Access is possible only through a low opening near floor level, requiring a person to crouch to enter. A second opening in the ceiling provides access to [[L_35 Unused Rooms|L_35B]]. Reaching it requires climbing through the deteriorated interior.
[[L_35 Unused Rooms|L_35B]] The upper room lies directly behind [[L_32B Interrogation Room|L_32B]]. Its position creates an unintended opportunity to listen to activity within the interrogation room.
