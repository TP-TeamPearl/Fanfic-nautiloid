# L_32B Interrogation Room
A small interrogation room adjoining [[L_32A The infirmary|the infirmary]], separated from the main workspace by a heavy curtain installed by the human crew to lessen the unease caused by its presence. The compartment was originally intended for questioning captured prisoners and suspected infiltrators recovered during the nautiloid's operations.
