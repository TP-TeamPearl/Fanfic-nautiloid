# L_27 The Garden
Illithid ribbed organic plating and dormant conduit veins remain particularly visible along the upper walls, suggesting that little of the chamber's original anatomy was altered. Whatever its intended purpose, [[CH_07]] claimed the room before departure and converted it into a private garden where he spends his free time—a privilege granted by [[CH_09 Gortash|Gortash]]. 
Artificial light sources provide sustained illumination in contrast to the vessel’s otherwise dim interior. Moisture levels are maintained through localized mist dispersal units adapted from medical sterilization equipment.
## Inventory:
- Ceramic pots from Baldur’s Gate
- Carved stone basins
- Aquariums filled with foreign substrates
- Empty jars
- Water Sink equipped with a shower
- Surface flora
- Potted orange tree
