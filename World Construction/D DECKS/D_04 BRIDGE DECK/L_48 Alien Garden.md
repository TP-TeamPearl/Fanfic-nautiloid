# L_48 Alien Garden
Its floor is covered with low, dark grass, with planters and stone shelving integrated into the curved hull walls. The shelves contain cultivated plants, fungi, and specimens of uncertain or planar origin.
The low ceiling and limited illumination make the room dim even when the vessel's artificial lighting is active. Moisture and organic decay are persistent features of the environment.
