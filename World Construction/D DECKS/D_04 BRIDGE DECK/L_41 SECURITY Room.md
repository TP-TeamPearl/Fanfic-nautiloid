# L_41 SECURITY Room
An isolated compartment has been carved into the thick shell of the nautiloid, forming a protected boundary between the bridge and the inhabited sections of the vessel. A connected iron doors secure the passage. Access is high above the floor.

This is the only known section of the nautiloid incorporating narrow reinforced windows directly into the outer shell. From within, the Bridge Deck can therefore be observed with the eyes.
A carved passage along one side leads first to an illithid resting place. Beyond it, the floor drops by a short step and the passage narrows beneath the ceiling, opening into a recessed compartment whose walls have been adapted into an enclosed shower. The arrangement is functional but distinctly illithid, with little separation between circulation space and personal facilities.

The opposite side of the chamber is occupied by a cluster of servers and associated equipment. Thick cables spill across the floor, forcing anyone moving through the room to step carefully between them. Beyond the equipment, a passage opens into a larger cavity containing an advanced control console.

The console occupies the broadest section of the room. Entering the control area requires descending several steps before reaching the seating position. The workstation follows the length of the adjoining observation window, allowing the operator to sit directly before the bridge-facing glass while monitoring the exterior.

# PI_22 Fire Suppression System
The primary control console is located in [SEC](<L_41 SECURITY Room>), it retains primary authority over the system.
A secondary remote control console is installed in the [Thrall Overseer's Office](<L_18A Thrall Overseer's Office>). 
Both consoles communicate with the central fire-suppression system through a combination of shielded hardwired connections and an independent resonance-linked wireless relay.

Critical communication and power connections run through reinforced, fire-resistant conduits. Ordinary branch cables and local connections remain vulnerable to damage.

The console provides three primary operational commands and a separate execution control:

**FIRE CONTAINMENT**
A thermal monitoring display identifies critical temperature zones and areas at risk of fire propagation. 
Upon activation, the system automatically evaluates the affected compartments and sends closure signals to the appropriate doors, establishing containment boundaries.

*Individual doors can already be operated independently. This function automates the assessment and selection process, allowing multiple doors to be commanded simultaneously without requiring an illithid operator to inspect each one manually (destroyed, obstructed or non-responsive doors may prevent successful containment.)*

**ISOLATE DECK**
Seals the selected deck from adjacent sections of the vessel.

This procedure closes available access points and interrupts non-essential connections and ventilation routes to prevent further propagation of fire, smoke or other hazards.
*Deck isolation is primarily intended to protect unaffected sections and critical infrastructure. It does not extinguish an existing fire.*

**INITIATE SUPPRESSION**
Activates the available fire-suppression measures within the selected compartments. 
The system evaluates the availability of the following resources before initiating the selected procedure.

Depending on the condition of the infrastructure, these may include:
- Activation of showers (water taken from [Water reservoirs (illithid)](<L_01 Water reservoirs Deposit# OI_03 Water reservoirs>) or [the Pools](<L_17 Pools# L_17 Pools>)).
- Air suppression.
- Deployment of available thralls (with smoke/powder spreader) for manual intervention when automated measures are insufficient and personnel can safely reach the affected area.

**EXECUTE / PURGE**
Once containment is settled through "containment" or "isolate".
This is a separate suppression procedure that must not be confused with the general "suppression" command.
