# L_41 SECURITY Room
An isolated compartment has been carved into the thick shell of the nautiloid, forming a protected boundary between the bridge and the inhabited sections of the vessel. A connected iron doors secure the passage. Access is high above the floor.

This is the only known section of the nautiloid incorporating narrow reinforced windows directly into the outer shell. From within, the Bridge Deck can therefore be observed with the eyes.
A carved passage along one side leads first to an illithid resting place. Beyond it, the floor drops by a short step and the passage narrows beneath the ceiling, opening into a recessed compartment whose walls have been adapted into an enclosed shower. The arrangement is functional but distinctly illithid, with little separation between circulation space and personal facilities.

The opposite side of the chamber is occupied by a cluster of servers and associated equipment. Thick cables spill across the floor, forcing anyone moving through the room to step carefully between them. Beyond the equipment, a passage opens into a larger cavity containing an advanced control console.

The console occupies the broadest section of the room. Entering the control area requires descending several steps before reaching the seating position. The workstation follows the length of the adjoining observation window, allowing the operator to sit directly before the bridge-facing glass while monitoring the exterior.
