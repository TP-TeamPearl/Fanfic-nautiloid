# L_50 Communication Office
The Communication Office occupies the center of a cultivated section of the vessel, isolated from the surrounding corridors by a winding approach. 
Thick conduit bundles cross the floor and ceiling in exposed runs, disappearing into fleshy bulkheads where engineered hardware interfaces with the vessel's nervous system. Rows of server pillars divide the room into narrow aisles, forcing the operators to weave between humming cabinets before reaching the operations desk. Although the infrastructure appears crowded and improvised, the workstation itself remains meticulously organized to support uninterrupted communications.

# PI_12 Frequency Cartography Array
(= Submarine Electronic Surveillance Receiver)

In [[L_50 Communication Office|L_50]] Continuously scans surrounding astral and planar bands for psionic, arcane, and mechanical emissions.
In its passive state, the array appears as a circular relief embedded into the wall—etched with concentric rings and fine geometric markings. Faint pulses move across its surface as frequencies are detected and logged.

When activated through a focused psionic gesture, the array unfolds onto the main desk as a three-dimensional projection:
A volumetric model of the surrounding astral field. The nautiloid's relative trajectory. Temporal layering of detected emissions. Signal decay patterns. Each detected frequency is represented as a shifting node within the projection. Two distinct tonal indicators accompany detection events:
- A short, ascending pulse when a new frequency enters range.
- A descending, muted tone when a signal fades or is lost.

## Frequency Amplifiers
A psionic transmitter (radio) module used to enhance and amplify psionic signals for communication and telepathy over long distances. Each amplifier consists of an elongated organic spindle surrounded by concentric metallic calibration rings engraved with illithid measurement marks. Suspended within the spindle. 
Operators adjust the rings using fine mechanical wheels and locking levers. The process resembles correcting a telescope or compensating for wind when aiming artillery: not because thoughts drift physically, but because the surrounding psionic environment constantly fluctuates.

Experienced communication officers learn to interpret these subtle instabilities almost instinctively. A poorly calibrated amplifier does not weaken a transmission so much as distort its intended destination, allowing thoughts to arrive incomplete, delayed, or to bleed into unintended recipients.

## Voice Box Console
The console's surface inset with four recessed slots designed to receive modular voice translation units. 

When active, the console channels directed telepathic speech into the selected module, which converts the signal into audible language through an integrated projection grille. The output is steady, neutral in tone, and devoid of emotional inflection unless deliberately modulated by the transmitting illithid.

Only two modules remain nearby: Common and Elvish. Previous language modules were removed or damaged during earlier campaigns.

### Voice Box: 
- Source:  The Illithiad
A psionically powered device, the voice box converts its wearer's telepathic speech into audible words in a language chosen when the item is made. Each device is a small, flat metal block, typically worn on a chain or pendant, and bears a line of qualith identifying its assigned language.

An illithid can wear the voice box around its neck or on a belt and activate it mentally. Once active, the device speaks aloud any telepathic message directed through it. Mental commands can also adjust its volume or shut it off temporarily. It can produce a strong voice, but not one loud enough to injure listeners in an enclosed space.
