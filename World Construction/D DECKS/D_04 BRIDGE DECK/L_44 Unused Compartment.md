# L_44 Unused Compartment
An unassigned compartment situated beyond the bridge's outer security door. Like the adjoining auxiliary deposit in [[L_43 Auxiliary Deposit|L_43]], the room opens directly onto the corridor without a conventional door, suggesting that it was intended for rapid access rather than long-term occupation.
No equipment has been permanently installed, and the chamber currently serves no operational purpose. Faint signs of biological reclamation are visible along the walls, though considerably less advanced than in the abandoned sections deeper within the vessel. Its continued accessibility suggests that the compartment remains structurally viable and may be reassigned should future requirements arise.
