# L_45 CH_07 & CH_06’s room
L_45 is the most refined crew accommodation aboard the vessel after the Captain's quarters. The room was negotiated by [[CH_07]] with [[CH_09 Gortash|Gortash]] and assigned jointly to [[CH_07]] and [[CH_06]].
The furniture is arranged in mirrored sections, dividing the room into two partially private areas while retaining a shared central workspace. One section also serves as a small office for [[CH_07]].
Its position and restricted access provide [[CH_07]] with additional assurance that [[CH_06]] remains in a secure environment.
