# L_47 Fresh Corridor
The rear extremity of the deck. L_47A is accessed through an iron door and contains no permanent furnishings. An open passage connects it to L_47B, a long corridor.
A breach in the outer structure allows natural light and unfiltered outside air into the rooms.
A passage at the end of the corridor provides access to the garden [[L_48 Alien Garden|L_48]], [[L_52 High Garden|L_52]] through a simple biological exclusion vestibule. Transparent flexible curtains separate the two sides, allowing crew to pass while limiting the movement of small animals and insects between the garden and the rest of the vessel.
