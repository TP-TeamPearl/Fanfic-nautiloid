# L_40 Balcony
L_40 is an exposed weapons platform accessed through the large forward opening of the vessel. The area provides direct visibility of the surrounding sky or astral environment but leaves thralls exposed during combat.
The balcony contains four arbalets positioned on either side of the bridge and canons. A Rib-Lattice Saturation Engine is mounted at the center of the platform. The weapon is operated remotely through psionic control from the weapons area [[L_76B Psionic Artillery Hub|L_76B]].

# WI_01 Cannon
Cannon is a living organism employed as one of the vessel's principal weapons. Resembling a large shelled mollusc supported by multiple arthropod-like limbs, it can move independently across the hull and anchor itself wherever required for firing.
The anterior portion of the creature forms a hardened organic barrel through which concentrated psionic projectiles are discharged.

**When inactive:** the organism retracts into its mineralized shell and adheres to the deck, passively absorbing ambient psionic radiation and regenerating. If energy falls below operational levels, partial fossilization increases its resistance to damage but slows activation. Regular biological maintenance is required to prevent over-mineralization and tissue degradation.

The maintenance station provides mechanical self-cleaning and controlled light exposure to stimulate regeneration. Due to the reduced crew during the mission, biological integrity remains under the Thrall Quartermaster's supervision [[CI_04]], while structural calibration and targeting alignment fall under SEC [[CI_02]].
*If poorly maintained, a cannon may fail to retract completely, leaving exposed tissue that secretes viscous fluid and requires Thralls to manually scrape away accumulated calcification.*

# SHIPBOARD WEAPONS 
- Source: Spelljammer Adventures in Space
A spelljamming ship typically has one or more ship board weapons, ballistae and mangonels being the most common. Such weapons are slow to load and fire. Player characters are almost always better off using their own weapons and spells in ship-to-ship combat, reserving shipboard weapons for targets that are too far away to be damaged by other means.
