## Elder Brain Transceiver
- Source: The Illithiad
When illithids must remain independent from their elder brain for extended periods, they may construct a stationary transceiver to extend their telepathic reach. Because the device cannot be moved easily, it is built only when they expect their base to remain in place.

The transceiver consists of a hollow, egg-shaped structure at least eight feet tall, with an entrance in its side or underside. Eight tentacle-like cables spread from the egg, supporting it before extending into eight shallow pools arranged around the device. Its crusted, organic appearance makes it seem alive, though the transceiver itself is not a creature.

To use it, one mind flayer occupies the hollow chamber while eight others stand in the surrounding pools. The eight channel their telepathy through the cables and into the egg, where the flayer inside combines and amplifies their power to establish contact with the elder brain.
