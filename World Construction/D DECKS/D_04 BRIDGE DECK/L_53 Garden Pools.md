# L_53 Garden Pools
Shallow pools and damp hollows are scattered throughout this portion of the garden. 
A concealed grotto is formed among the rocks at the edge of the garden.
On either side of the entrance, two stone seats identical to those found in the crew cabins L_12 are integrated directly into the walls. Their placement faces toward the pools beyond.
## Inventory:
- Shallow garden pools
- Hidden rock grotto
- Two stone seats
- Small aquatic and amphibious organisms
