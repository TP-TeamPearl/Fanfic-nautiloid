# L_43 Auxiliary Deposit
A secondary storage area occupying part of the bridge deck's principal access corridor. Unlike the vessel's native architecture, the arrangement is widely regarded as a consequence of hurried Banite logistics rather than deliberate design. 

*Although this security issue has been noted and is expected to be corrected during the vessel's first major refit, it remains a recurring source of irritation among the illithid crew.*
