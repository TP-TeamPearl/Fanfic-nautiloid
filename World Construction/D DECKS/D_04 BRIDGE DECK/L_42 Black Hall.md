# FI_08 Cannon Maintenance Station
Occupying the inner side of the bridge deck, this native biomechanical installation is arranged around a central organic structure from which four recessed cradles radiate, giving the apparatus the appearance of a broad "X". Each cradle contains a softly illuminated cavity sized to receive a single resting [[L_40 Balcony# WI_01 Cannon|Cannon]].
The station serves as a maintenance and regeneration point rather than a storage rack. While docked within a cradle, a cannon remains partially integrated with the vessel's living tissues, allowing nutrients and energy to be exchanged as damaged structures regenerate in preparation for redeployment.
Should the station become unavailable, the creatures simply anchor themselves elsewhere upon the bridge, although regeneration proceeds more slowly without the specialised support provided by the cradles.
