# L_54 Bio-technical Workshop
A narrow passage leads from the garden [[L_53 Garden Pools|L_53]] toward a small enclosed room. The garden grass ends abruptly at the threshold, leaving the floor beyond covered instead by the vessel's exposed structural material and fragments of broken stone, metal, and organic matter.
The room was repurposed in haste, a human-built water filtration assembly occupies most of the available space, with its pipes and conduits connected to the vessel's broader water network.

# FH_03 Water Filtration Assembly
Installed in [[L_54 Bio-technical Workshop|L_54]] prior to departure under Banite engineering directive, this apparatus operates independently of the nautiloid's organic water cycling system. Condensed moisture is drawn through a multi-stage purification chamber before being stored in reinforced containers in [[L_48 Alien Garden|L_48]] reserved exclusively for human consumption.

The installation was considered necessary because the human crew neither understood nor trusted the illithid method of collecting and processing water.
