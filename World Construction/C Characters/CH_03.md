# Background
| Origin: | Neverwinter |
| ------- | ----------- |
CH_03 is a Gondian artificer [[Gond# History|taken into Gortash's service]] alongside many other members of the community. His family is unusually large, giving Gortash considerable leverage over him; their continued safety is sufficient to ensure his cooperation.
Gortash selected him for the Nautiloid as a docile and experienced artisan whose absence from the Foundry would be tolerable. His age made him valuable for the practical knowledge accumulated over decades, but comparatively expendable to the long-term operation.

*CH_03 understands that he is being used as a disposable specialist in a prototype project whose success matters more than the people assigned to it.*





# Personality
Suggested: Neutral Good
His age and experience give him strong social awareness. He usually sees tensions before they become conflicts and knows when to step back, soften a disagreement, or leave someone alone. He rarely gives unsolicited advice; people tend to seek him out when they want it.

He is one of the safest people aboard to approach with a problem. He does not insist on authority, rarely judges, and does not exploit personal information shared with him.
The crew treats him as a mixture of senior craftsman, grandfather and reliable colleague.
He tends to help by asking questions and making things work rather than telling people what they should do.
