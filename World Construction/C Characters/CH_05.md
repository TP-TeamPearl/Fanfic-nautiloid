# Bond with [[CI_05]]
**Bond ambiguity:** The exact mechanism by which [[CI_05]] established the cognitive bond should remain unknown. Neither conventional enthrallment nor simple telepathic domination fully describes it.

[[CH_05]] and [[CI_05]] developed a gradual cognitive bond through shared work, repeated meditation, and psionic reinforcement. It is not possession, communion or partnership in the human sense. 
The bond is experienced as clarity rather than coercion.

[[CI_05]] does not supply her thoughts or commands; it keeps her cognition within patterns compatible with its own objectives.
[[CH_05]] retains self-awareness and can initiate decisions, but her range of viable projections has narrowed. Independence remains possible in theory; however, every future she imagines without [[CI_05]] produces a version of herself she considers less capable, less precise and intellectually inferior. Her conclusion is therefore not: "I cannot leave", but: "I would be less".

**Psychological Condition:** Her intellect has become sharper through prolonged exposure to illithid cognition, but ordinary human conversation increasingly feels inefficient and imprecise. Her isolation is therefore primarily **cognitive rather than emotional**. 

**No privacy, no solitude:** Solitude is not entirely restful, meditation serves as maintenance and alignment rather than relaxation.
