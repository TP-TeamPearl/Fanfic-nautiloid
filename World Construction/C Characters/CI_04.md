[[CI_04]]: illithid specialized in thrall management. It possesses a remarkable understanding of thrall psychology and behavior, particularly among humans and kuo-toa. It possesses a unique blend of compassion and cruelty (Soviet-inspired methods), employing a twisted approach to assert control over its prisoners and minions.

It takes the time to understand each individual's unique strengths and weaknesses, leveraging this knowledge to maximize their efficiency aboard the vessel as it does within the colony.

Its affection creates emotional dependency and dangles protection like a gift.

# Thrall relationship
Through the Nourisher Creed, [[CI_04]] has the authority to decide **who is worth keeping**. It can recognize an unusual specimen and reserve it rather than allowing another illithid to consume it. It can also set aside exceptional thralls as calculated gifts to illithid associates. 

It knows its thralls individually: their strength, endurance, reflexes, useful instincts, tolerance for pain, preferred stimuli, weaknesses, and the particular conditioning that produces the best response. 
It remembers which captive showed promise during intake, which one failed, which one is better suited to controlled entertainment (arena), and which one is simply better used as nourishment. 
**It does not regard the thrall pool as an undifferentiated mass.**
Its pride comes from knowing its stock intimately. It can look at a newly captured humanoid and begin assessing what that body could become under proper conditioning. Likewise, it can immediately recognize when another illithid has wasted a useful thrall through careless treatment or excessive punishment.

**Fragrance-like:** Nothing visible but perceptible at close range to nearby illithids. Other thralls are obedient to their masters and share a special bond to them, but their responses are most of the time flat, mechanical, and briefly heightened by whatever stimulus is currently being applied. [[CI_04]]'s thralls carry something more persistent—a faint emotional residue associated with recognition, love, comfort, anticipation, devotion. An illithid in close proximity can sense that _these_ thralls belong to [[CI_04]] in a more intimate way.

# Nourisher Creed
- Source: The Illithiad
The Nourisher Creed believes that an illithid community's thrall population is its most precious resource. Nourishers treasure their thralls above all else and do not take for granted the many chores that thralls undertake for the improvement of the illithid race.

Of course, all illithids recognize the importance of thralls; the Nourisher Creed merely seeks to promote the significance of the thrall in contrast to all the other special interests and Creeds on which other illithids attach their hopes. Nourishers often maintain the facility that houses the general thrall pool; this Creed's devotion to the thralls seems most suited to such duties.
