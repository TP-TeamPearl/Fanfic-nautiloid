# Social Dynamic
CI_02 is restrained and efficient. Very patient also, a calm explanation is often cheaper than dealing with the consequences of poor understanding. That patience could make it seem more approachable than it actually is.

**Communication exists to produce alignment.** 
It keeps interaction with other illithids to a minimum unless communication is necessary to accomplish a task. Conversation without an operational purpose should feel especially inefficient for it, adding that prolonged social exchange introduces ambiguity without necessarily producing a useful result.
**Social enough:** It is is capable of directing people, maintaining discipline, explaining procedures, negotiating practical disagreements and persuading others when action is required. It simply does not attach much value to the social process surrounding those actions. (Keep it 'other', like Scaevola in Mechanicus)

CI_02 is an engineer by function and outlook.
At the colony, its work was constrained by circumstance. Shar's curse reduced contact with the outside world, merchants became rarer. This forced it toward maintenance, instead of designing ideal systems, it learned to keep imperfect ones operating through repair and re-purpose.
Improvised solutions had to remain stable long enough to become permanent ones.

This experience reinforced a preference already compatible with its Ariser outlook: **systems should be made resilient to environmental constraints rather than allowed to fail because conditions are unfavorable.**

# Ariser Creed
The Ariser Creed holds that illithid domination must eventually extend to the surface, even if sunlight makes doing so difficult. In their view, confining themselves to the subterranean world means surrendering any realistic prospect of ruling the planet in its entirety. They reject the Darkeners’ strategy of waiting for the sun to be extinguished, arguing that such a delay could leave the illithids unable to act in time—or worse, that the Darkeners might never succeed at all, condemning the species to an unrealized destiny.

Much of Ariser activity therefore centers on finding ways for illithids to function with minimal impairment under full daylight. They also envision enclosed surface settlements: illithid colonies protected from direct sunlight by domed structures.
