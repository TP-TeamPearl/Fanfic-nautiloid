# Background

| Origin: | Underdark (?) |
| ------- | ------------- |
[[CH_08]] belongs to minor Lolth-aligned house, but was raised largely isolated from the Underdark's social world. They know the basics of Drow hierarchy, Lolth, slavery, and the major Underdark peoples, without possessing the cultural polish or political instincts of a typical noble. Their house eventually ceased investing in them, and they have operated independently in Amn ever since.

Isolation and repeated failures have tempered their arrogance, and as their life is almost entirely their work, they find little interest in noble etiquette or maintaining social appearances. 
## OLD Partnership with [[CI_00|The Emperor]]
Built on mutual utility and repeatedly validated by results. [[CH_08]] was not a "prime ally" but one worth keeping as they produced specialised, difficult-to-duplicate goods. The Emperor provided access to discreet buyers, distant markets, and secure channels unavailable through ordinary trade.
### Boring details
The arrangement was efficient and deliberately low-friction:
- CH_08 supplied rare goods and technical expertise.
- The Emperor provided access, protection and distribution through the Knights of the Shield.
- Profits returned to tools, research and further production.

They never met in person. *[[CH_08]]'s shifting identities and distance would have prevented the Emperor from reliably locating them, and its capture by Gortash disrupted its operations before it could look into the matter.*

What made the Emperor exceptional was the quality of its calculations. It brought order to complex undertakings, maintained a stable direction through changing circumstances, and consistently calculated toward the strongest outcome or the smallest acceptable loss.
Other partnerships could provide useful resources or connections, but none reproduced the same combination of order, foresight, and consistency. 
And if [[CH_08]] was already capable of building and maintaining their own operations, the difference was scope: with the Emperor, their ambitions could become larger and more concrete. It gave them a glimpse of what a sprawling, optimised system could look like, something desirable enough that participating in its construction became an objective in its own right.

When the Emperor stopped answering letters, CH_08 initially assumed betrayal or death. Rumours eventually connected the Knights of the Shield to a renegade illithid and its disappearance to Moonrise.
