### Assignment
Mandated aboard the nautiloid directly by Gortash. He did not volunteer.
Gortash nevertheless granted him an unusual concession: a private chamber with no standing mission-critical assignment during off-duty hours ([[L_27 The Garden]]). CH_07 treats this as both recognition of his value and confirmation that he was right to consider himself difficult to replace.

*He knows exactly how much influence Gortash's favour gives him and is not shy about using it.*
# Social Dynamic
Charming, talkative and eccentric. Often annoying, rarely malicious.
He is socially careless: makes demands without noticing their burden, assumes others will understand his priorities, and can become argumentative when challenged by people he considers less competent.
He enjoys exposing mistakes with a caustic amusement that can sound cruel even when he has no intention of humiliating anyone.

Most crew members therefore treat him as:
**useful, difficult, entertaining, and best approached when he is in a good mood.**
He is generally not hated. His expertise is too valuable, and when he chooses to help, the result is usually worth the trouble.

**[[CH_08]]:** "Unfortunately, they are very good."
A capable asset with useful foreign experience and an irritating tendency to question assumptions. Enjoys provoking them into demonstrating what he knows.

### Relationship with [[CH_06]]
Father, mentor and long-term research partner.
He taught her through direct practice, expecting her to learn by doing rather than by following rigid instruction. He sees competence as something earned through repetition, failure and adaptation.

His affection for her is genuine but often expressed through work: teaching, trusting her with difficult tasks, correcting her technique and quietly preparing for dangers she has not noticed.
He becomes markedly more careful when her safety is involved.
### Working Relationship with [[CH_04]]
[[CH_07]] defines the biological and cognitive problem (brain); [[CH_04]] solves the physical engineering's one (machine).

*Their collaboration should therefore be frequent and slightly abrasive:*
> [[CH_07]]: "I need the system to recognise this signal" 
> [[CH_04]]: "Then tell me what the signal looks like"
> [[CH_07]]: "If I knew that, I wouldn't need you"

CH_04 can physically modify, bypass, isolate, reinforce and document the machinery while CH_07 determines what those changes need to accomplish biologically. That would also give CH_04 a reason to become unusually knowledgeable about the prototype helm bridged systems. He is not merely "helping the scientist"; he is gradually learning the machinery alongside him.

# Personality
Suggested: Chaotic Neutral
His public manner suggests a man who does whatever he wants.
In practice, he is highly controlled.

The eccentricity is deliberate enough to function as freedom: he refuses to let other people's expectations dictate what deserves his attention.
He considers his discipline of mind to be one of the few things separating him from the ordinary mass.

### Working Style
[[CH_07]] is exuberant during experimentation and genuinely delighted by new results. His fascination is contagious, even when his methods make others uncomfortable.
He does not deliberately cause suffering when it can be avoided, but he does not conceal disturbing procedures merely to make them easier for others to witness. To him, discomfort is not an argument against useful work.

He is meticulous beneath the theatricality. His apparent spontaneity is supported by strict personal discipline, extensive preparation and unusually high technical standards.
He expects himself to meet standards he does not necessarily expect from others.

### The Garden
While preparing for the expedition, [[CH_07]] deliberately changed direction and began cultivating plants rather than devoting every spare hour to technical research.
His private chamber became a compact garden containing exotic plants, flowers and useful specimens adapted to artificial conditions.

The garden is not simply another laboratory. It is one of the few things he tends without trying to optimise immediately.
He experiments occasionally with extracts, soils, growth conditions and unusual botanical reactions, but the act of keeping something alive has become a personal interest in its own right.

**Phase_2:** The garden later becomes entangled with the nautiloid's [[S_017# S_17B|biological contamination]].
