### Relationship with Other Illithids: 
**No ambition:** It does not seek status among the other illithids. This makes it difficult to manipulate through privilege, insult, flattery or competition.

[[CI_05]] is foreign-colony stock, but its origin does not make it an outsider in any fundamental sense. It remains part of the same unified illithid community, regardless of which elder brain currently holds its allegiance.

It was assigned to the colony precisely because of its qualifications: an exceptionally capable psion with methods and knowledge developed elsewhere. Its continued loyalty to its distant elder brain creates a degree of political ambiguity, but not necessarily distrust. It is here to strengthen their operation, and it does so competently.

### Relationship with [[CH_05]]
[[CH_05]] functions as an interface between [[CI_05]] and the human operational environment, but not merely as an "administrative" translator.
Through her position, [[CI_05]] gains legitimate access to human facilities, personnel, documentation, supplies and infrastructure that would normally remain outside an illithid's permitted sphere of activity. What is formally assistance is therefore also an expansion of its operational reach.

[[CH_05]] provides the human-facing authority required to enter laboratories, request materials, consult records, move between restricted workspaces and interact with personnel. [[CI_05]] can therefore observe and influence parts of the colony that would otherwise remain inaccessible or heavily contained.

The arrangement benefits the mission, but also gives [[CI_05]] considerably more room to pursue objectives beyond its declared laboratory duties…

Dispatched by its elder brain to intervene in a failing illithid operation beyond its home colony. The intervention failed, and political constraints prevented its return.

# Personality
Extremely restrained, patient, and difficult to read. Not domineering or argumentative. 
**A near-perfect bureaucrat:** capable of accepting a subordinate role, tolerating humiliation or personal disadvantage, and allowing undesirable outcomes when they preserve the larger structure or serve continuity. A slight is ignored if retaliation is unnecessary; status matters only when it affects the plan.

**Cold in discipline, but not emotionally empty.** Its restraint comes from patience alone. An Awaiter is expected to spend weeks or months arranging conditions and contingencies, then taking genuine pleasure in watching the structure collapse exactly as anticipated. 

The **Abysmal Creed** influences its methods: terror is useful, and it understands the value of reminding humanoids what an illithid is. Its eldritch aura provides a more immediate pleasure—the simple, direct enjoyment of watching fear and discomfort spread around it. But cruelty and terror remain instruments and pleasures within a broader philosophy of calculated action, rather than its primary doctrine.

It rarely needs to threaten, persuade, or argue. Once the pieces are in place, circumstances can be arranged so that the desired outcome becomes inevitable, and it can simply watch.

# Misc
Its danger comes from the amount of preparation that can remain invisible until it acts.
### Communication
Rarely communicates directly with humanoids unless necessary. Unfiltered interaction with inferior minds is considered inefficient and potentially contaminating; [[CH_05]] is the deliberate exception imposed by circumstance. Rather than accept the limitations of human communication directly, it has gradually conditioned and refined her into a sufficiently precise interface for the work required of them.
*[[CH_05]] is useful because she can translate between its cognition and the human systems surrounding them. She is not a preferred companion, trusted confidante, or developing attachment. Were a more efficient interface available, [[CI_05]] would have little reason to retain her.*

When direct communication occurs with the humankind, it is brief and rarely verbal. It prefers vivid, invasive or unsettling mental images and sensations over words.

Since the chosen took control of the colony, experimental reports must be rendered in Common and preserved in formats usable by humanoid personnel. [[CH_05]] therefore translates their procedures, observations, and results into human documentation, serving as its practical interface with human administration.
### True Objective
[[CI_05]] continues its mission despite the disruption of the original operation by the Chosen .
Its priority is **systemic persistence**: preserve the conditions necessary to eventually complete its objective, even if personnel, alliances, command structures or the mission itself change.

# Creeds
- Source: The Illithiad
## Awaiter Creed 
The Awaiter Creed values deliberate, coordinated planning over rapid action. Its illithids carefully consider every relevant factor before committing to a course of action, believing their kin are often too impulsive. They seek understanding through patience and calculation amid the constantly changing multiverse.

To the untrained mind, the cosmos appears chaotic, but Awaiters believe an underlying Law imposes order upon that change. They tend to remain impartial to other philosophies beyond that of calculated action, and are often chosen by the Elder Concord for aedileator roles because of their measured nature. Yet their patience also makes them the architects of some of the most elaborate and sinister illithid schemes.
## Abysmal Creed
The Abysmal Creed believes illithids should deliberately cultivate the fear and revulsion other races already feel toward them. By making their threat unmistakable, they reason, other peoples will become more willing to submit to illithid rule and accept their status as thralls.

Most illithids reject this philosophy, though it occasionally gains influence. When Abysmals shape an operation, they favor methods designed to cause maximum terror and horror among those affected.
