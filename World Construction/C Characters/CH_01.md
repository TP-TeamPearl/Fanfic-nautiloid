# ｡･ﾟ☆ ──────  Background story ────── ★ﾟ･｡ 

She left her plane out of simple curiosity, eager to discover what the world beyond her home had to offer. With little idea of where she was going or what she was supposed to do, she simply wandered wherever her curiosity took her.

The people she met seemed to appreciate her beautiful voice, so she became a bard, singing and playing music along the way, earning just enough to get by and continue her travels. Unfortunately, her talent for finding herself in the wrong place at the wrong time eventually led her to Moonrise Towers. She now contributes in the only way she knows how: singing, playing music, and trying to keep everyone's spirits up after a long and rather unpleasant day.

She's not entirely sure how she got here or what she's supposed to do next, but she's seen worse and isn't particularly afraid of what's to come...

# ｡･ﾟ☆ ──────  Personality ────── ★ﾟ･｡

❀  _A romantic trouble-magnet with absolutely no survival instincts._ ❀ 

On one hand, she's clumsy; on the other, she's truly unlucky. She can be perfectly graceful and clever, yet somehow always ends up in the middle of the one conversation she shouldn't overhear or the one room she absolutely shouldn't enter. When someone makes a mistake and has the good sense to flee, she'll simply stay behind! lost in contemplation! and end up taking the blame!

She is sentimental, easily fascinated and she finds the world endlessly interesting.
She can be quite clever but only when it truly matters. She is not naive, but she has a stubborn tendency to believe that there is something good and worth loving in almost everything.

**ੈ♡ Her particular contradictions ♡ੈ** 

❀  fascinated by the world but becomes terribly anxious when surrounded by crowds.
❀  loves meeting people but is terrible at remembering names.
❀  gentle but also stubborn.
❀  sometimes she wants calm and to be alone but regret it the second silence occurs.
❀  She is sentimental about objects that other people would consider rubbish.
❀  She wants to constantly  make new friends but tend to neglect the ones she already has

## ｡･ﾟ☆ ──────  Things she likes ────── ★ﾟ･｡

❀  Flowers. Flowers growing in unexpected places, very druid-like compatible
❀  the smell of wet earth of rainy afternoons
❀  the smell of old ornamented books
❀  lanterns and candlelight
❀  Strangers' stories. Especially when people tell her about their childhood, their hometown or someone they miss
❀  Tea!
❀  Birds. She can sing and dance with them. She loves even insects like moth, beetles and all forest tiny creatures like frogs and snails
❀  Small acts of kindness. She remembers them for an unreasonable amount of time

❀  being treated like a child!
❀  seeing flowers deliberately picked and immediately discarded
❀  People who are cruel to animals!
❀  Being rushed
❀  Unnecessary violence and loud arguments
❀  People who destroy beautiful things for no reason
❀  dry environments
❀  Being ignored while speaking
❀  Bitter food
❀  Extremely crowded places

# :｡･ﾟ☆ ──────  Little habits and peculiarities ────── ★ﾟ･｡

❀  She names plants she encounters and she occasionally gives unsolicited names to things
❀  She keeps little objects in the folds of her robe: stones, feathers, buttons, coins, pieces of ribbon ...
❀ She collects interesting words and occasionally uses them in completely inappropriate contexts
❀ She's very tactile and  has a habit of touching beautiful things before asking permission
❀  She gets distracted in the middle of conversations by things happening in the background.

# ｡･ﾟ☆ ──────  Social ────── ★ﾟ･｡

With strangers: Friendly, curious, sometimes a little too comfortable. She asks questions that people normally wouldn't think to ask.

With friends: Affectionate, attentive and occasionally clingy. She sometimes brings people little gifts that have no monetary value whatsoever.
With people she loves: She remembers everything. Their favourite drink, the way they laugh, the little things they complain about. 

With authority figures: Respectful until she becomes confused, which tends to happen quickly
With enemies: She would much rather talk first. She is not particularly fond of hatred and often struggles to understand why people are so committed to it.
