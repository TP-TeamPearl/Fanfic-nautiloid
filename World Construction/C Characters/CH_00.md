Suggestion: Lawful Neutral

The Captain is rotting in his own skin. The ship is not his. His presence aboard the nautiloid is ceremonial—a relic in a cockpit built for creatures who think faster than he can react. His commands feel redundant. Even the console defers to the copilot when he fails to translate the systems in time.
He keeps a flask hidden in the armrest. It spilled its contents. The console sparked. He didn't fix it.

**Captain — Established direction**

- Has an uncomfortable but recurring acquaintance with Quenora Grisly; is accustomed to the Elfsong Tavern (but has no prior acquaintance with the KotS.)

1. The captain operates a merchant vessel in the sword Coast under contract to an Upper City noble (Sea of Swords, Shining Sea).
2. The noble has commercial dealings with Gortash's increasingly powerful network.
3. The captain accepts a lucrative transport contract involving sealed experimental reagents. He knows their dangerous properties, but neither the noble or he knows about their purpose or that they origin from Moonrise's labs.
4. An accident destroys his vessel and cargo. He saves most of his crew, but loses his livelihood and remains financially liable.
5. The noble offers to settle his obligations, but Gortash sees an opportunity for his  experimental nautiloid crew in preparation and refuses to release him.
6. The captain is sent to negotiate directly with Gortash. He accepts command of a spelljammer to settle his obligations and restore his professional standing.
7. He learns the details of the mission and the nature of the ship and the other crew once in the colony.
