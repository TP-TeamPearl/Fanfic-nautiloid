When Shar's curse and the elder brain's dormancy made the colony's inherited methods insufficient, the [[CI_03## Creative Creed|Creative]] Creed expanded its research toward the arcane structures already embedded in the human world. What healthy colonies would normally reject as inferior or contaminating became, in Moonrise, a necessary avenue of experimentation.

CI_03 therefore became unusually versatile without abandoning its original specialization. It learned to recognize where arcane systems could complement, translate, or extend psionic principles, even when the two could not be reduced to the same framework.
This singular development is now exploited by Gortash aboard the Nautiloid. CI_03 serves as a bridge between paradigms, working especially closely with [[CH_03]], whose expertise in arcane artificing provides the practical counterpart to its deeper psionic craftsmanship.

## **Space Observation**  
CI_03 maintains an extensive study of the sky and the wider planes, mapping not only celestial bodies but shifting routes, anomalous regions, artificial structures, portals and other disruptions to the expected geometry of space. In a world where gods and archmages can alter reality, old maps are hypotheses rather than certainties.

Its interest extends to the large-scale movement of resources between worlds, the viability of distant colonies, major transport routes, and recurring threats such as piracy, monsters or unstable magical regions. It does not follow politics in detail, retaining only the major movements that alter the broader system.

To CI_03, this knowledge could eventually support the Grand Design: knowing where colonies can draw resources, how they can remain connected, and which distances or phenomena must be overcome for an empire to function across worlds.

# OI_15 Wild Space Orrery
Inside a Wildspace system, this portable arcane device automatically tracks the positions and movements of all suns, planets, moons, and comets within that system, projecting a display of all these bodies in the space above its current location. In that display, a white, pulsating pinprick of light marks the orrery's location. 


## Creative Creed
- Source: the Illithiad
The Creative Creed is made up of illithids devoted primarily to investigating and developing new psychic disciplines, psionic artifacts, and technologies. Its members see continual innovation as essential to the advancement of the species and ultimately to its mastery of the universe. For many, however, the immediate appeal is more personal: the excitement of experimentation, discovery, and uncovering something genuinely new.

Ideally, the Creed operates a central research laboratory within its community, giving members a place to conduct both individual and collaborative work. The facility also preserves the results of earlier experiments, including unsuccessful ones, in sealed vaults for storage and future study.
