### Living / Resting Habits
Despite having an assigned rest room in [D_04 L_52](<L_52 High Garden.md>), [[CH_02]] dislikes having a predictable location.
He Keeps his actual resting place uncertain, favouring concealed passages, maintenance spaces, dead ends, and difficult-to-access areas. This is partly security instinct, partly habit: he is more comfortable knowing how to disappear than knowing where he will sleep. Currently occupies [D_03 L_37A](<L_37A Old Deposit.md>).
# Misc

According to Gruumsh, the evil gods who created the other races did so to serve their own purposes. As a result, these races possess innate characteristics that reflect the nature of their creators.
Most orcs inherit the violent and savage nature of their patron deity, Gruumsh, which naturally inclines them toward evil. Even those who choose to follow a good alignment must struggle throughout their lives against these inherent tendencies. Half-orcs, too, remain affected by the lingering influence of the orc god.

## The Nautiloid as Wilderness 
He treats the nautiloid less like a ship and more like an unexplored ruin / hostile ecosystem. He respects living systems, but does not trust the nautiloid. Its biology feels wrong to him: rooms disappear, flesh consumes old structures, passages change, and its internal rhythms resemble a creature rather than a machine. He gradually treats the vessel like a sick animal.

## Notebook and reports
[[CH_02]] is perfectly capable of producing a conventional report. His preference for remembering locations through stories, landmarks and sensory associations is simply his own method of organising information. The difficulty is to **translate** it, since Gortash does not need to know how he remembers the vessel, but how to use it.

- Personal field notebook (to himself)
  His observations, route mnemonics, sketches and recollections. (Difficult to interpret because the notebook's organisation follows his own mental associations.
