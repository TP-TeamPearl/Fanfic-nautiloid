# Background

| Origin: | Moonrise         |
| ------- | ---------------- |
| Creed   | Influencer Creed |
The Emperor is a renegade illithid suffering from a rare and severe form of partialism—retaining its original memories, likely its soul, and the same burning desire for freedom as the sailor it once was. It deceived the entire colony by behaving like any other thrall until it was granted an opportunity to scout outside. From there, it found assistance and returned home—to Baldur’s Gate.

Little is known about the centuries that followed, but its later activities reveal a consistent pattern. The Emperor infiltrated existing power structures rather than opposing them directly. It approached Duke Stelmane, who held influence both within the ducal council and the Knights of the Shield, a powerful mercantile network operating in secrecy. Initially acting as an asset, it gradually took control by impairing Stelmane through psionic means, using her as a public interface while directing operations from behind the scenes.
Through this position, the Emperor shaped both political influence and illicit economic networks, operating indirectly while remaining physically hidden beneath the city. Its psionic influence extended through intermediaries whose loyalty was beyond reason, eventually drawing the attention of Gortash. As his ambitions expanded, they came into conflict with existing powers in Baldur’s Gate, leading to the discovery of the Emperor’s hideout, its capture, and its return to a colony now controlled by the Chosen.

# Phase_0
During Phase_0 (before the vessel's departure) the Emperor affiliates with the [[CI_00# Creed - The Influencers|Influencers]] while already positioning itself for a higher role within the Elder Concord. Its unusual status makes it valuable to some illithids, who may see it as a source of knowledge or an opportunity to exploit its singular experience. 
Others are likely to regard it with suspicion: a former renegade who escaped this very same colony, deceived its former peers, and may carry the risk of contaminating the collective through its remnants of humanity.

The colony's weakened state leaves room for this normally abnormal arrangement. The elder brain has only recently emerged from dormancy, conventional authority has been disrupted, and much of the colony's effective administration now falls under human control. The resulting instability should allow the Emperor to gain influence unusually quickly, even as it remains reluctant to accept the limits of that authority and continues seeking greater opportunities.
# Creed - The Influencers
- Source: The Illithiad
The Influencer Creed concerns itself with the unearthing of secrets and the swaying of opinions through indirect methods. According to an Influencer, riches, psionic power, and military might avail nought in the absence of exact information regarding a specific goal.
Such information gathering goes hand-in-hand with the creation of a web of influence which best channels the direction of an enemy's response to perceived threat. Many a thrall city has fallen due to insufficient preparation in the face of an illithid threat; the lack of preventative measures in these cases falls solely at the feet of Influencer agents.
Influencers often lead vigileator intelligence gathering operations. Influencers normally maintain a small complex where Creed members train in the arts of infiltration and spying.
