
# What Gortash wants 
## from CH_02

**1: This particular nautiloid.**
Gortash needs accurate information about its current configuration: dimensions, passages, access points, machinery locations, room connections, modifications and functional limitations… everything that the banites or the illithids have not reported before the departure or that may be subject to change during the travel.

**2: General nautiloid principles.**
Gortash wants to identify characteristics that can be applied to other vessels, even when their internal configurations differ.
Rooms can be slowly reclaimed, organic structures can change, machinery can be added or adapted, and different vessels may have different histories.
