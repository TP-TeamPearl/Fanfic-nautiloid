### Role
Specialist in necromancy and invasive experimentation. Her work concerns what can still be extracted, preserved, altered or made functional after death.
Once a subject is dead or no longer suitable for living experimentation by [[CH_07]], it may be transferred to [[L_65 Necrotic Lab]].

She maintains undead biological components, studies post-mortem neural activity, and develops methods for integrating dead tissue with psionic and mechanical systems. Her work provides the biological component of Gortash's remote-control prototypes.

### Specialisation
- Necromantic preservation through psionic means, including localised reanimation of dead tissue for further psionic or mechanical experimentation.
- Post-mortem study of brains and nervous tissue using delayed-effect reanimation magic.
- Soul extraction, preservation and containment.
- Remote post-mortem mind control: testing necro-psionic control of dismembered or otherwise separated bodies after death.
- Integration of undead biological material with arcane machinery and modified illithid devices.
- Surgical alteration (led by [[CI_05]])

### Individual Research

[[CH_05]] conducts part of her research independently in [[L_61 Bioculture Chamber|L_61]].

Material transferred from [L_65 Necrotic Lab](<L_65 Necrotic Lab.md>) is kept under long-term observation to study:
- survival and degradation of treated tissue;
- microbial colonisation and decomposition;
- reactions between necrotic material and living cultures;
- delayed effects of necromantic or psionic intervention;
- whether biological function can persist after conventional death.

Her work in is slower and more observational than her surgical work. She records changes over days or weeks, compares samples, and tests whether effects observed in the laboratory remain stable outside the original procedure.

She is particularly interested in the interval between **death, reanimation, and irreversible decay**: what remains functional during that transition, and what conditions can extend or alter it.
