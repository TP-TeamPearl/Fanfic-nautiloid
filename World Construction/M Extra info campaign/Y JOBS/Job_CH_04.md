Primary engineer and human specialist for machinery. Responsible for maintaining, repairing and reverse-engineering human and hybrid systems.


**Illithid Machinery Documentation:** documenting and analyzing illithid machinery and technology encountered aboard the nautiloid. This involves carefully observing and sketching machinery, noting down its functions, components, and any unusual properties.

# Relationship with the Vessel
[[CH_04]] has an unexplained sensitivity to the nautiloid's machinery. He notices changes in rhythm, vibration and behaviour that others miss, sometimes understanding that something is wrong before he can explain why. He sometimes talks quietly to mechanisms as though listening to them.
> *"The ship is alive. I can hear it breathe."*
