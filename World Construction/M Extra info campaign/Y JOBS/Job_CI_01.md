# Role Aboard the Nautiloid

[[CI_01]] serves as a **psionic relay and communications specialist**, acting as a bridge between the nautiloid crew and Gortash's Black Hand organization.
Its particular value as a relay is interpretive rather than technical. [[CI_01]] does not merely transmit a message; it determines what the message means, what emotional state produced it, and which cultural assumptions are embedded within it.

# CI_01 — Detection and tracking

First pass: Operates the Frequency Cartography Array and monitors detected emissions.

- Detects presence outdoor.
   Establishes preliminary profiles from detected signatures.

- Calculates and tracks trajectories.

- Filters routine signals from potentially anomalous or dangerous activity.
  Transmits relevant contact data and flagged anomalies to [[CI_02]] and the appropriate operational offices.
