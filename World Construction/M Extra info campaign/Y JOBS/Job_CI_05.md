
Continues the biological and necromantic research established at the Moonrise colony. 
Although [[CI_05]] is officially [[CH_05]]'s [[Job_CH_05|laboratory assistant]], it silently directs most procedures. Their coordination appears effortless, the result of extensive repetition and psionic synchronization.

The [[TOOLS## Tentacle Extensions|Tentacle Extensions]] equipment allows [[CI_05]] to perform several manipulations simultaneously through its four tentacles, leaving [[CH_05]] with a limited but meaningful share of the procedure. This gives her room to develop her own technical competence while keeping [[CI_05]] in practical control. *From the outside, the pair simply appears exceptionally well coordinated.*

The two volunteered for the nautiloid and continue their previous working arrangement with little interest in the wider crew.

# Psionic Reservoir Contributor
Due to reduced illithid staffing aboard the vessel, and its non-critical operational role, [[CI_05]] is designated to be daily [[L_12 Psionic Drain cabins#L_12 Psionic Drain cabins|drained]] from its psionic energy and refill the [[L_08B Psionic Corner# OI_04 Psionic Reservoir|Psionic Reservoirs]] of the vessel.
This allocation is treated as routine optimization rather than disciplinary measure, despite its cumulative impact on cognitive endurance.
