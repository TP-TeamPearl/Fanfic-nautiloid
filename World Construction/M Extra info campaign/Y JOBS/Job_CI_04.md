# Quartermaster
Before departure, CI_04 is responsible for making sure the thrall population is viable for the mission, but it likely also (likely?) handles the practical needs of the human crew: clothing, hygiene supplies, food, bedding, tools, replacements, basic personal equipment, medical necessities, and whatever mundane infrastructure prevents a long expedition from becoming dysfunctional.


# Thrall relationship
Through the Nourisher Creed, [[CI_04]] has the authority to decide **who is worth keeping**. It can recognize an unusual specimen and reserve it rather than allowing another illithid to consume it. It can also set aside exceptional thralls as calculated gifts to illithid associates. 

It knows its thralls individually: their strength, endurance, reflexes, useful instincts, tolerance for pain, preferred stimuli, weaknesses, and the particular conditioning that produces the best response. 
It remembers which captive showed promise during intake, which one failed, which one is better suited to controlled entertainment (arena), and which one is simply better used as nourishment. 
**It does not regard the thrall pool as an undifferentiated mass.**

Its pride comes from knowing its stock intimately. It can look at a newly captured humanoid and begin assessing what that body could become under proper conditioning. Likewise, it can immediately recognize when another illithid has wasted a useful thrall through careless treatment or excessive punishment.

The affection of its thralls should feel real **within the narrow emotional capacity left to them**. Its conditioning leaves behind a particular positive response attached to its presence, something distinct from a Resonance Stone. When it approaches, they may become more attentive, more eager to comply, and display small signs of pleasure or relief. A thrall that is otherwise vacant may brighten subtly in its presence.

**Fragrance-like:** Nothing visible but perceptible at close range to nearby illithids. Other thralls may be obedient to their masters, but their responses are most of the time flat, mechanical, and briefly heightened by whatever stimulus is currently being applied. [[CI_04]]'s thralls carry something more persistent—a faint emotional residue associated with recognition, comfort, anticipation, devotion. An illithid in close proximity can sense that _these_ thralls belong to [[CI_04]] in a more intimate way.
