### Role on the Nautiloid
Bridges human machinery with illithid psionic systems. Maintains, adapts and improvises interfaces where neither technology functions cleanly on its own.

[[CH_03]] also works directly on Gortash's prototype machinery, particularly devices intended for future nautiloids and human operators. Some are designed for use on distant worlds and other planes, where human technology must function under unfamiliar physical, magical or environmental conditions.
The voyage therefore serves as an unusually valuable testing ground: astral travel, planar transitions and alien environments expose the prototypes to conditions impossible to reproduce reliably in a workshop.

The crew itself is part of the experiment. Engineers, arcane artisans and illithid specialists work in unusually close proximity, allowing failures to be identified and corrected across disciplines rather than in isolation. [[CH_03]] is one of the principal figures translating between these fields and turning experimental solutions into workable systems.
