# Synopsis — BG Canon

When Shar's curse starved the colony, the elder brain withdrew into slumber beneath the future Moonrise Towers. Unknown to those above, it endured in dormancy. In time, it subtly reached the Chosen Gortash through dreams and guided him toward placing the Karsus Crown upon it, binding the elder brain beneath the Chosen's will.

The Chosen's plan was not to restore the colony for its own sake. They intended to infest Baldur's Gate with Netherese tadpoles, halting ceremorphosis while creating a network of enthralled worshippers. The colony therefore revived, its master stronger than before, but shackled to a greater purpose.

# Colony Status

The Chosen prioritize strategic objectives over day-to-day discipline within the colony.
## Phase 0 — Pre-Board
## Mind reading & Preparation
In Moonrise, every worker knows the unspoken rule: if protocols falter, your thoughts will no longer be your own. An illithid can strip them bare, turn them into chains, or reshape them into tools.
Even attempting to deal with one as a partner carries its own risk. They may pry, tempt, manipulate, or bind, probing for whatever weakness they can exploit. They are thralls of the Chosen, but not brain dead.
The colony is not in crisis, secrecy, or collapse. It exists in a prolonged state of **controlled saturation**.

Multiple authorities coexist without trust, but with enforced compliance. The Chosen dominate through command priority rather than constant intervention. The Elder Brain perceives everything relevant, but intervenes only when a threshold tied to a strategic objective is crossed. Banite officers enforce discipline obsessively where they have the capacity to do so, but they lack the manpower and attention to correct every deviation.

As a result, the colony operates under **permissive brutality**. Unauthorized experiments, minor theft, resource misuse, informal exchanges, gambling, drinking, personal projects, and social friction are commonplace. None of this is particularly shocking. Nothing is truly private either, but most things are beneath notice.

**Visibility is cheap; attention is expensive.**

The colony therefore strips privacy without stripping agency. People remain capable of making choices, forming relationships, taking risks, breaking rules, and pursuing personal objectives. They simply do so within an environment where an illithid may be watching and where intervention is highly selective.

Relationships and emotional states are largely irrelevant to the authorities unless they produce consequences. As long as no one causes murder, sabotage, serious disruption, or interference with nautiloid deployment, behavior is generally tolerated.

Illithids are present as functionaries, overseers, researchers, and collaborators rather than as a unified cultural bloc. Their internal hierarchies, Creeds, rivalries, and ideological judgments remain significant to them, but they do not dominate everyday life in the nearer surface levels of the colony.

### Temporal Condition

The pre-board phase is temporally open-ended. No nautiloid has returned, so there is no corrective feedback from an expedition and no pressure to consolidate what has been prepared.

Preparations therefore continue without a firm endpoint. Accumulation, bargaining, informal arrangements, and personal positioning all make sense; decisive action does not.

Everyone is waiting.
No one knows for how long.
## Moonrise Towers — Surface
Ketheric Thorm remains the visible authority. He deals with travellers, merchants, and pilgrims drawn to the Absolute's promises. Surface activity appears ordered, disciplined, and religious. The reality beneath the towers is considerably less controlled.
## Necromancers — Colony
The necromancers are not tadpoled and are fully aware that the Absolute is the elder brain. 
Their research combines necromancy with psionics.
With [[PHASE 0 - The Colony#^disap|Balthazar absent]], oversight is weak. Unauthorized experiments and personal projects are common. Resources are misused; test subjects are neglected or spoiled. Scholars linger in corridors, drink, gamble, converse with illithids, and pursue work unrelated to their official assignments.
Informal pairings develop. Certain human necromancers repeatedly work with particular illithids, while illithids likewise develop stable operational partnerships through repeated collaboration ([[CH_05]] & [[CI_05]]). Such arrangements are officially discouraged but practically tolerated.
## Core Principle
The Elder Brain does not openly rebel, lie, or disobey.
It **steers**.
Through omission, timing, selective intervention, and tolerated disorder, it incrementally shapes conditions toward a single eventual outcome: 
**One Netherstone lost is enough to create a viable path to freedom.**

The Elder Brain operates within the boundaries imposed upon it while exploiting every ambiguity those boundaries permit. Through dreams, it quietly introduces the idea of Orpheus—the gith prince capable of nullifying the hive's song—and maneuvers others toward conditions in which the Astral Prism can enter its reach.

## Overall Atmosphere

- **Chaotic but productive.** Research and preparations advance, but through fragmented, unauthorized, and overlapping efforts.
- **Minimal enforcement.** Banite officers are stretched thin; necromancers exploit their freedom; Bhaalists introduce additional volatility.
- **Muted tension.** Tadpoles cloud the minds of most cultists and thralls, allowing contradictions and dysfunction to persist without producing constant resistance.
- **Controlled neglect.** The Elder Brain allows substantial dysfunction because the colony continues to fulfill its strategic purpose.
## Banites — Gortash's Officers

The Banites are primarily concerned with equipping and preparing a prototype nautiloid.
They are overworked. Maintaining the vessel and getting it ready for deployment takes precedence over policing the rest of the colony. When they encounter disorder, they may yell at necromancers or cultists, but they rarely have the time or manpower to investigate further.

The prototype will conduct trial runs with a **non-enthralled human crew** composed of scientists and engineers brought from Baldur's Gate. Their purpose is to test Gortash's machinery under alien conditions. The results, successful or otherwise, will inform the design and construction of future vessels.
## Bhaalists

Bhaalists are present in smaller numbers and support necromantic research through ritual refinement.
They introduce additional instability into the colony. They move relatively freely and often operate without sustained oversight.

## True Souls — Ketheric General Workforce
The general cultist workforce is tadpoled and stationed throughout Moonrise and the upper colony levels. Their minds are clouded. Their obedience is therefore not absolute discipline so much as **a lowered capacity to recognize that anything is wrong**.

- **True Souls:** The absolute highest tier of authority below the Chosen trio. They host an Illithid tadpole, granting them psychic control over everyone below them. **Disciple Z'rell** holds this rank.
- **Acolytes:** Entry-level cult initiates. They handle basic auxiliary tasks and magic support under the supervision of higher-ranking True Souls or necromancers.
- **Adepts:** Mid-tier casters. You will encounter specialized **Adept guards** casting support magic, crowd control, and visual surveillance spells around the tower's choke points.

- **Myrkulytes:** Ketheric's personal, fanatical necromancers loyal to Myrkul (secret affiliation, none is tadpoled).
- **The Ironfinger / Breakers:** Specialized absolute shock troops (often absolute-aligned duergar or goblins) tasked with breaking slave spirits or handling heavy munitions.
