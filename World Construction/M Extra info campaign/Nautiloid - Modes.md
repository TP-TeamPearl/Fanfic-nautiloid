# Cruise (Mode)

The standard operating configuration for sustained navigation.

The pilot maintains the vessel's intended trajectory (established by [[L_50 Communication Office|COM]]) through its connection to the spelljamming helm, combining the Nautiloid's [proprioceptive feedback](<The Nautiloid>) with its own cognition and available external observations.

**Operational characteristics:**

- Smooth displacement and gradual course corrections.
- Economical propulsion and ordinary machinery operation.
- Continuous assessment of position, orientation and movement.
- Autonomous minor corrections within sufficiently clear surroundings.
- Active pilot supervision and intervention when circumstances require.

Cruise is the default configuration when no specialised operating mode is required.

# Pursuit (Mode)
(Bird of prey / Escape the meteorite field)

Suitable for short periods of intense activity rather than extended cruising.
Pursuit Mode permits much more aggressive manoeuvres:

- Abrupt acceleration and deceleration.
- Sharp changes of direction and steep inclinations.
- Short, powerful bursts of propulsion.
- Rapid trajectory corrections and evasive manoeuvres.
- More aggressive use of the vessel's tentacles and other capture systems, where appropriate.

**Limitations:**

- High consumption of engines (and energy reserves).
- Increased demands on the pilot's concentration and neural connection.
- Greater mechanical and biological strain on the vessel.
- Reduced suitability for prolonged operation.
- Increased detectability compared with low-emission configurations.

# Drowsing (Mode)
(= Snorkel Bill)

A low-activity operating state allowing the Nautiloid to recover its internal resources and restore its biological and psionic systems.

The vessel naturally favours this state in dark environments. Exposure to strong light reduces the efficiency of the recovery process.

**During this state:**

- **Ventilation:** Ventilation systems increase circulation through the vessel's living tissues. Stale air can be purged and/or recycled more efficiently. 
- **Water systems:** Biological filtration and moisture extraction operate at increased capacity, allowing the reservoirs to replenish more rapidly.
- **Psionic reserves:** The vessel's own psionic energy can regenerate.
- **Psionic-dependent Machinery:** Nonessential systems are reduced or placed in standby to minimise unnecessary activity.

The exact configuration depends on environmental conditions and the vessel's current requirements.

**Limitations:**

- Recovery is slower under strong illumination.
- Water replenishment depends on available moisture.
- The vessel's reduced activity limits operational responsiveness

The pilot should be able to interrupt the process and restore normal operating configuration when necessary.
