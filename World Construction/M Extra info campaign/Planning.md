# Planning

The nautiloid follows a **rolling 24-hour cycle**, not a fixed shipboard day. 
(CHECK! Disabled→ Artificial lighting creates a "night" period for the human crew; illithids retain their own wakefulness cycle and remain responsible for critical vessel functions.)

## Standard Cycle
- Bound to: [[L_57 Secretariat# PI_01Clocquartz|PI_01 Clocquartz]]
**Cycle:** 16 h active/free-duty periods + 8 h rest.

| Period                   | Duration | Conditions                                                                            |
| ------------------------ | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Intensive Work**       | 5 h      | At assigned post; primary duties; sustained concentration.                            |
| **Work**                 | 5 h      | On duty; may relocate, perform secondary tasks, coordinate, inspect, or maintain.     |
| **Restricted Free Time** | 2 h      | Remain at post; may eat, relax, converse quietly, or sleep without disturbing others. |
| **Free Time**            | 4 h      | Free movement through all accessible areas; normal security restrictions apply.       |
| **Principal Rest**       | 6 h      | Critical personnel.                                                                   |
| **Nap / Meditation**     | 2 h      | Critical personnel; meditation for illithids and elves.                               |
| **Principal Rest**       | 8 h      | Non-critical personnel, including artisans; replaces the 6 h + 2 h rest pattern.      |
