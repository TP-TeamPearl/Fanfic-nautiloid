- **Sphincter access is not rank-based.** 

Sphincters have no rank-based restrictions and respond only to beings capable of providing the required psionic input. Illithids are naturally linked through the hive mind and are presumed to act in the colony's collective interest. As a result, they can freely operate the vessel's biological interfaces, which recognize them as authorized users. Access restrictions are therefore primarily designed for the human crew rather than the illithids themselves.

*Human crew can theoretically bypass this limitation through an appropriately calibrated seal, but illithids are reluctant to grant such authorization as the areas beyond these doors generally contain systems outside the humans' assigned duties and often beyond their technical comprehension.*
# Rank_0
**No Rank (R_0 Id):** Prisoners lack the psionic tattoos required to interface with control panels and cannot operate psionically keyed mechanisms, including lift controls and reinforced access doors.  While escorted, they may be transported via the Lift. Passage through veiled access points is possible only when an illithid escort actively suppresses the field for the duration of transit.

# Rank_1 
**Captain (R_1 Id)**: A human title of authority specific to this nautiloid. The rank carries substantial authority over the human crew but comparatively little weight among the illithids themselves.


> [!NOTE] **Illithid Authority:** 
> Illithids remain subject to the Elder Brain and, through it, to the authority of the Chosen and their appointed officers. Aboard the nautiloid, the captain therefore possesses legitimate authority over the illithid crew despite being human. However, illithid priorities remain vessel integrity, mission completion, and obedience to the captain, in that order. 
> 
> The elder brain's influence weakens with distance. This does not normally allow an illithid to openly disobey a valid command, but it gives them greater room to interpret orders, identify loopholes in protocols, and pursue their own interests. At sufficient distance, the human command structure becomes increasingly dependent on the illithids' willingness to cooperate. Attempts to undermine or remove human authority therefore become a genuine, though normally suppressed, possibility.

# Rank_3
**Specialized personnel (R_3 Id):** Personnel with access to technical and controlled areas relevant to their work, including laboratories, resource management, and other sections containing valuable equipment or sensitive information.
# Rank_4 
**Second-class workers (R_4 Id):** Non-critical personnel with very limited access. They are generally confined to their assigned workspace and basic facilities such as restrooms.
# Rank_5 
**Expendable personnel (R_5 Id):** Essentially thralls. Their access is limited to basic functions, primarily the Lift and its associated controls, allowing them to move through the vessel when required.
