Crimson Draughts is an apothecary and Araj's house, localised within the Lower City in Baldur's Gate. 
The first floor of the building resembles a long-neglected storage, while the small room on the ground floor has a workbench with some alchemical ingredients and a locked hatch, leading to the basement, which houses Araj Oblodra's laboratory.
The store is owned and run by Araj Oblodra, a Lolth-Sworn Drow merchant and alchemist. She can be met in Moonrise Towers.
