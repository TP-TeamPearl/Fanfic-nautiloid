# Local Rule

- **Dominated**  
    A sentient's will has been temporarily overridden by an illithid. The master can impose simple instructions and compel the subject to perform straightforward actions or other tasks within the subject's ordinary abilities (see lore about "charm").
    
    The effect is inherently unstable and may weaken or break through injury, interference, or other circumstances. When the control is lost, the sentient regains their free will and may remember at least some of what they were compelled to do.
    
- **Thrall**  
    A thrall is the result of a deliberate indoctrination process that entirely reshapes the subject into an obedient and permanent servant of the illithid hierarchy, even if their master dies. Creating a thrall requires significant time, attention, and psionic energy (see 5e lore).

## Bond
- (Goal is to stick to the lore but cues are scarce, feel free to correct and enhance)
The affection that thralls have for their master should feel real **within the narrow emotional capacity left to them** (CHECK! lore: I found only “double agents acting normal but without any cues about their feelings” and “hollow husks,” such as the Cook in BG3).

Their conditioning may leave behind a particular positive response associated to the master’s presence, something distinct from a [[L_08A Resonance Stone Corner# OI_09 Resonance Stone|Resonance Stone]]. When the master approaches, they may become more attentive, more eager to comply, and display small signs of pleasure or relief. A thrall that is otherwise vacant may brighten subtly in their presence.
