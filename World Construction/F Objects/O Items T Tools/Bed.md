


# OH_07 Metalic Bed
The standard bed model aboard is inspired by carceral design. Made from a corrosion-resistant, easy-to-clean metal alloy, it prioritizes durability and practicality over comfort. Its rigid, unforgiving surface requires thick wool blankets to provide any degree of warmth and cushioning.
# OI_07 Stone Bed
Illithid bed. The capsule bed is rectangular and made of stone. Its lateral opening reveals a gently sloped interior lined with dense, dark moss that maintains constant moisture, with faint pooling along the lower channel. A cool vapor lingers within the enclosure, preserving the occupant's skin without saturation. 

Beneath the pod, a shallow recess is cut into the stone to store personal implements, while a flat upper ledge provides additional space for stocking eventual heavy items. The model is uniform and unadorned, devoid of ornament or softness. Through sustained contact between flesh and living hull, the resting illithid remains cognitively tethered to the vessel's pulse, suspended in regulated stillness rather than true sleep.
