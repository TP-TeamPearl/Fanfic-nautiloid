# OI_08 Brain Jar
Preserved brains are stored in reinforced containment units commonly referred to as Brain Jars. Each contains a living humanoid brain suspended in stabilized cerebrospinal fluid. When compromised or destroyed, the fluid rapidly degrades into caustic brine, producing both corrosive residue and toxic vapors.

Brains designated for use with the Mind-Archive Interface are catalogued and stored in a secured compartment (designated [[L_23A Illithid Kitchen|L_23A]]). Selection is governed by mission relevance rather than origin; recent subjects are often preferred due to clarity of recall, though this practice remains controversial even among illithids.
## Balthazar's Notes - Location: Mind Flayer Colony
- Source: BG3
Experimental notes written in Balthazar's precise hand.

His obvious deficiencies aside, it cannot be denied that young Gortash has the mind of a scientist. Before his latest invention, the illithid library was no more than rows of brains swimming in the cerebral slop. With his device, we no longer need psionic power to access the memories contained within - only to insert the subject and speak to it directly. I do wish he were not so eager in testing the device on more recent subjects of his own, however - nor in leaving their skull shavings scattered along my workbench.
Various 'minds' in green jars can be found and inserted into the Mind-Archive Interface, which lets the player character have a conversation with the unfortunate creature via the Slack-Skinned Head.
