
# TI_01 Cephalometer
Metallic, psionically empowered device that directly measures the head sizes, psionic potential and chemical balance of thralls via two inwardly curved projections connected at one end. The Qualith readings appear on a small blank panel on one arm of the cephalometer.

# TI_02 Tentacle Extensions
Used on this nautiloid by [[CI_05]] to practice surgery more comfortably.
## Tentacle Extensions
- Source: The illithiad
Tentacle Extensions: While unattached, tentacle extensions (grouped in arrangements of four or six) appear as damp cables 3 feet in length; these extensions often have razor sharp blades attached to one end. A small duct within the extension also conveys an illithid's natural flesh-dissolving enzyme to the bladed extension tip.

illithids cannot wear tentacle extensions for more than two hours at a time before debilitating fatigue sets in. Once a mind flayer removes an extension, it cannot activate that particular device for another 12 hours.
# TI_04 Examination Slabs
Each slab contains shallow grooves that channel fluids toward embedded reclamation drains. Flexible restraint bands—organic in appearance but reinforced internally—allow subjects to be immobilized without complex mechanical devices.

*These slabs are used for dissections, special tadpole implantation procedures, and neurological extraction.*

# TI_05 Suspended Instrument Rack
Rack of articulated arms holding surgical implements, psionic probes, and neural extraction tools. The arms move through subtle psionic commands, allowing an illithid to operate multiple instruments simultaneously without touching them.

*To human observers the instruments appear almost autonomous, drifting slowly into position as needed.*
