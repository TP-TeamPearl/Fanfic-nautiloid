## Qualith
- Source: Volo's Guide to Monsters
Qualith is the written language of mind flayers. A writer imprints their thoughts into intricate four-line patterns, employing a tentacle or other appendage on each of the four lines. Nonillithids may attempt to decipher it and extract occasional cues, but prolonged effort is more likely to result in a severe headache.

*The illithids may have little intrinsic need for physical documentation, but far from their elder brain and under Banite protocols ([Record](Protocols.md##%20Record%20Everything)), they are coerced into keeping physical traces of their operations beyond the standard security measures they already apply.*

# OI_10 (Rewritable) Qualith Tablet
A stone support used by illithids to transcribe their thoughts.

**Rewritable model:** Made from a specific stone originating in the Outer Planes, its surface can be cleared by controlled abrasion and rewritten several times. Each treatment gradually erodes the stone, eventually leaving the tablet too thin and fragile to remain usable.
