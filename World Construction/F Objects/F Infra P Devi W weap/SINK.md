# FI_06 Sink_A
Dissolution basin: Embedded seamlessly into the wall at waist height, the Dissolution Basin resembles a shallow stone and flesh basin filled with a dense, dark liquid. The surface appears still, almost lacquered, yet any material introduced into it slowly loses structure—organic matter first softening, then fragmenting, before vanishing entirely beneath the opaque surface. 

No visible drain exists. Instead, a subtle inward pull suggests internal circulation, drawing dissolved matter through concealed channels into the vessel's deeper containment system (Sanitary Receptacle Unit). The basin emits little sound and no visible vapor. It is not designed for hygiene but for reduction. Objects placed within are not discarded; they are unmade.

# FI_07 Sink_B
The Fountain. Dissolution pool: A bigger Sink_A emerging from the ground and present only in [[L_58B The Fountain|L_58B]]. The dark fluid circulates slowly, thick and viscous, releasing intermittent bubbles that burst with faint, wet reports. The air above it carries a heavy, acrid odor. Larger biological remnants are deposited here, where gradual agitation accelerates dissolution before transfer to the vessel's containment pouch. 

When large organic mass is committed to the Fountain, pale vapor rises in thin sheets. Within it, faint bands of green-blue light flicker and collapse, resembling a distant aurora trapped in a well.
