# PI_06 Birthing Pod
A moss-lined containment pod generally used during ceremorphosis. Once a tadpole has been implanted, the host is sealed and restrained inside while the transformation runs its course. The restraints prevent the convulsing subject from injuring itself or interfering with the process, while the pod allows illithids to monitor and tend to it throughout the transformation.

## Pod
- Source: BG3
Narrator: *The device releases a stream of fragmented memories and emotions - all that remains of the pod inhabitant's former self.*

- Source: The Illithiad; Lords of madness
Ceremorphosis is a brutal, week-long transformation. After a tadpole is implanted, illithids drag the quivering host into one of the birthing pods in a moss-lined chamber, where it undergoes the process in relative privacy, gradually changing from humanoid to illithid. 

The victim suffers delirium, violent convulsions, and other disturbing symptoms as the brain is gradually consumed. A mature illithid and one or two thralls supervise the host, restraining it when necessary and cleaning it during brief periods of calm.
Because new mind flayers are produced only rarely, these chambers are usually empty. They can hold several hosts at once, but that is uncommon.
Each birthing pod sports thick moss and smells strongly of cinnamon and garlic.
