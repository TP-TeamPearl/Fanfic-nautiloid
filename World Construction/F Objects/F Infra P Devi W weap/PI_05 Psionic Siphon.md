
# PI_05 Psionic Siphon
The Psionic Siphon (aka "the Lamp") is a ceiling-mounted extraction device designed to recover surplus psionic energy from resting illithids. Suspended on an articulated support, the emitter can be positioned directly above the occupant's head before activation. During operation, its central disc emits a faint violet radiance while drawing excess psychic output from the subject and transferring it through the vessel's conduit network into nearby psionic reservoirs.

The extraction process is passive and intended to occur while the occupant rests or meditates. It causes little discomfort beyond mild mental fatigue if prolonged, allowing otherwise wasted psychic activity to be reclaimed for the operation of the nautiloid's biological systems. The articulated assembly allows the emitter to be retracted against the ceiling when inactive, leaving the cabin unobstructed.

*Newly created illithids are routinely assigned to siphon cabins until their transfer to a colony, making them a continuous source of energy during the voyage.*
