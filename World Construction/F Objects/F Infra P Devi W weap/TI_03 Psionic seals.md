## Crew Seal Imprinting
Before departure, the illithid security officer [[CI_02]] reviews the finalized operational schedule with the human officers. Once the assignments are accepted, the security officer prepares the [[L_57 Secretariat# PI_01Clocquartz|Clocquartz]] and begins the imprinting sequence.

The underlying seal patterns are prepared by an illithid trained in psionic circuitry and crafting [[CI_03]]. CI_02 is not necessarily capable of designing every seal independently; its role is to select the appropriate pattern, associate it with the crew member's rank and duties, and calibrate it to the Clocquartz's operational cycle.

The procedure is performed individually inside [[L_57 Secretariat|L_57]]. Each human officer is required to present themselves physically before the machine under the supervision of CI_02. The resulting psionic seal establishes the individual's schedule, identification, and authorized access to designated parts of the vessel.

The thralls assigned to the voyage undergo the same process, although their existing markings may be partially removed or overwritten before receiving the seals appropriate to the new vessel. 
These markings may incorporate information identifying their originating colony, assigned authority, or other data required for coordination between allied nautiloids. 
Such records remain part of standard departure procedure even when no inter-vessel cooperation is presently planned.
