# WI_03 Psionic Jammers
The vessel's jammers reduce its detectability to Gith patrols in the Astral Plane, but also disrupt long-range telepathic communication. Illithids must therefore communicate face-to-face or use the monitors and a coded gesture language, hijack the communication room's vocal apparatus to broadcast an audible message across the vessel, or send a Thrall as a messenger. The jamming field is also strong enough to interfere with Gith psionic abilities while they are aboard.

The jammers include advanced visual and auditory sensors as well as psionic detection systems, allowing them to monitor both physical intruders and anomalous psionic activity throughout the vessel.

-  They are activated before crisis—not during—unless the disruption provides a specific tactical advantage.
