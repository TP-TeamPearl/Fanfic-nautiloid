# FI_04 Archival Substrate Array

Function: Local data retention and conditional archival relay
Location: 

A series of fixed server pillars composed of mechanical memory cores interlaced with organic conductive tissue and crystalline storage matrices. Unlike traditional illithid knowledge storage, which is predominantly psychic and centralized, the array provides a local, non-psychic means of recording and preserving data captured by the different office.

● When an event meets a predefined condition, the relevant data are transferred to the [[L_41 SECURITY Room|SEC]]. main servers. 

The servers are physically distributed throughout the room and connected through exposed conduits running beneath the floor, across the ceiling, and into the surrounding vessel structure. Individual nodes may differ in internal configuration according to their assigned role, capacity, or connection. 

The array is therefore treated as a single system composed of multiple archival nodes rather than a collection of identical machines.
