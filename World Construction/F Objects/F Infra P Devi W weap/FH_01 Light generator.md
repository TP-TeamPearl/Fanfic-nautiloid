
# FH_01 Light generator
- Tech: Gortash and Balthazar
A receptacle within a machine, designed to accommodate a Pixie within a glass container, submerges the mechanism into a liquid while in operation. The resulting light is then conducted through the cables within the nautiloid.

The nautiloid possesses no native lighting infrastructure. Illithids require none, relying on darkvision and psionic perception, and the vessel's organic tissues emit no illumination suitable for sustained work. 
*Historically, localized fire sources and bioluminescent tanks were sufficient for limited operations, as evidenced in older configurations. With the presence of a distributed human crew, however, such measures proved inadequate.*

To resolve this, a centralized illumination generator was installed in Light Room [[L_38 Light Room|L_38]], a secured compartment inaccessible to both unaware and unauthorized personnel. The system is based on a prototype developed through joint engineering principles attributed to Gortash and Balthazar. At its core is a modified containment housing designed to receive and interface with a pixie lantern of the type used to counteract the Shadow Curse.

The lantern's output is amplified and redistributed through a network of concealed conduits, illuminating key operational zones throughout the vessel. The thralls' deck and the bridge receive the highest and most stable levels of light, as human crew requires consistent illumination to function effectively, and command areas must remain readable by human operators. 
*Secondary areas may receive reduced output or intermittent lighting depending on system load.*

The pixie contained within the lantern is kept in a restrained state, her energy continuously siphoned to sustain the system. Maintenance of the containment unit, including feeding and stabilization, is assigned to [[CH_05]]. The process causes ongoing distress to the pixie; however, her vocalizations are largely suppressed by the surrounding machinery and the constant resonance of the engines.

Knowledge of the generator's true nature is restricted. The human crew believes the system to be an arcane-mechanical hybrid powered by an unknown planar source. Only a select few are aware of the sentient component involved.
