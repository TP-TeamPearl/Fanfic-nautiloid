# WI_03 Psionic Jammers

The vessel's psionic jammers reduce its detectability (to Gith patrols) in the Astral Plane by disrupting its psionic signature.

The interference field also disrupts Gith psionic abilities and long-range telepathic communication. While active, illithids must rely on face-to-face communication, monitors and coded gesture language, the communication room's vocal apparatus for vessel-wide audible broadcasts, or messengers such as Thralls.

-  They are activated in dangerous areas to avoid detection, before crisis—not during—unless the disruption provides a specific tactical advantage.
