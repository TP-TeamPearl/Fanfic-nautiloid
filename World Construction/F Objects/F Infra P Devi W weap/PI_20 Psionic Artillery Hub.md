# PI_20 Psionic Artillery Hub
Seat used by a mind flayer to load psionic energy into weaponry outdoor the nautiloid and attack enemies through them.
Near its foot rests a broad conductive plate intended to discharge static electricity. Behind the seat, heavy blocks are connected by thick cables to the surrounding hull, forming part of the system's energy transfer apparatus.

*A metal arc rises behind the position of the occupant's head. On either side, circular modules flank the head and establish the primary psionic connection. No conventional restraint is provided. *

Once the operator settles into the seat, thin red, vine-like tendrils emerge from the structure, coil around the forearms, and pierce the veins; its purpose is to establish direct circulation between the operator and the vessel. The operator's hands rest against translucent fluid-filled panels integrated into the armatures. These conduits connect to the vessel's psionic reservoirs in [[L_08B Psionic Corner|L_08B]], allowing the seat to draw stored energy and transfer it into the [[WI_00 Rib-Lattice Saturation Engine|Machine-Gun]]. 
*The illithid therefore does not normally expend its own reserves to operate the remote weapon system. Instead, the vessel supplies the energy while the occupant provides the cognitive and psionic control required to direct it.*

- Through the connection, the occupant perceives the surrounding space according to the vessel's own sensory data. Hostile vessels appear less as clearly rendered objects than as shadows or dense silhouettes within the surrounding void. The system can convey their approximate position, distance, mass, and the thickness of intervening structures, making it effective for detecting and targeting nearby vessels.

The interface is considerably less effective against highly mobile targets. Fast-moving enemies, particularly githyanki mounted on red dragons, can change position too quickly for the transmitted perception to provide reliable targeting.

Use of the system requires substantial illithid psionic aptitude. The seat supplies power and sensory information, but the operator must retain the transmitted data in their mind while simultaneously shaping the remote weapon's response. It is therefore a system designed specifically for illithid cognition rather than a conventional control station that could simply be operated by any crew member.
