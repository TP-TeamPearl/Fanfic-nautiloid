# PI_18 Scrying eye
- Source: BG3
A Scrying Eye resembles a floating, lidless glass eye used by the Cult of the Absolute for remote surveillance. It observes its surroundings and relays what it perceives to a distant receiver, allowing personnel to monitor locations without being physically present.

# Scrying eye
Our Scrying Eyes can be directed through psionic commands and require little independent decision-making. They possess no additional security of their own and will obey anyone capable of establishing the appropriate psionic connection.

CRISIS: During an emergency or when an area cannot be safely inspected, an Eye can be activated and detached from its mounting, then directed psionically through corridors and adjoining rooms. This allows the illithids to spies or inspect potentially dangerous compartments without exposing themselves directly.

*The illithids have to retrieve the EYE physically after a given it a task if psionic aboard is nullified.*
