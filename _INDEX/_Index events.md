# Phases & Events
**"Phase_x"**
The story is divided into four major acts, called **PHASES** to distinguish them from the canon campaign.
- **Phase 0:** Before departure. Focused mainly on the colony, character introductions, the beginning of their quest, and the foundations of social dynamics.
- **Phase 1:** Work organization aboard the ship, emergence of the first tensions, secrets, and consolidation of social dynamics.
- **Phase 2:** Increasing environmental oppression; established structures begin to break down (sanity decreases, illithids exploit protocols, etc.).
- **Phase 3:** Artifact acquired, gith pressure, and crash.

**"Px_xx"** is incremented by 5 by default, as we do not yet know how many events there will be.
They represent the major events within a **PHASE**.

→ They also act as filler or as the main storyline. Their .md file contains *our canon story*, which may be an existing character POV, a combination of several POVs, or something unique. These are likely to be the final pieces of the project, so we may favor a single writer to keep the writing style consistent.
# Scenes
**"S_xxx"** is a scene ID. 
**They do not necessarily follow chronological order.** Scenes represent the point of view and interactions of different characters during an **EVENT**. The same scene may be written by different writers for different characters without issue.
*Characters may be wrong or lack information. These gaps contribute to shaping future tensions within the crew.*

**Chained scenes
Alphabetical order is preserved** within a chain. 
Chained scenes do not receive a unique ID; they reuse the ID of the original scene followed by a letter. The original scene must therefore be MANUALLY updated with the letter **A**. 
For example: `S_12` → `S_12A` → `S_12B`
`S_12B` may also be introduced in a later PHASE. If a new scene is inserted into an existing chain, you may need to MANUALLY shift the following letters from **B** through **Z**.

- Overlapping chained scenes should remain separated, add a mere link like "- **Bound to:** [S_xxx]"

A system color may be used to indicate the importance of a scene.
